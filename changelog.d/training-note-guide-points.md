# Training's language note, and Calibration Guide points when a beacon moves

### Fixed
- **Training says which parts are still in English.** In German, French and Dutch, the note now says the walkthroughs and parts of the Quick Start and Full Manual are English, instead of only saying the help is translated.

### Changed
- **Moving a beacon keeps its Calibration Guide points.** Beacon Tune's hard relocation deleted them with its own points. New Guide points now stay, like Pin & Listen and Roam points. If a moved beacon keeps showing at its old spot, delete them by hand. Guide points saved by 0.38.209 to 0.38.216 are still treated as Beacon Tune points.

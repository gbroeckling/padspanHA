# Problems in plain words

### Changed
- **Health's "Problems found" now says what you would notice, in plain words.** Each check used to read like an engineer's log ("LOO mean error: 1.85 m", "Average RSSI variance 31.0 dBm²", "Scanner disagrees with consensus", "bidirectional transitions"). Now the title says what you'd see ("Kitchen and Hall get mixed up", "Receiver “Hall proxy” often points to the wrong room", "Signals in the Kitchen keep changing"), the message says why and the action what to do. The figures are all still there, on a "Details:" line under each card. The kinds and how serious each is are in words too ("Floor plan", "Receiver", "Signal and learning"; "Fix this", "Worth fixing", "Good to know"), and a floor is named the way Home Assistant names it, not by its id.
- **A new house is told its next step, not a critical problem.** With no calibration yet, Health said "No calibration data" as a critical problem, though rooms already come from the nearest receiver. It now says "Next step: calibrate". A floor plan uploaded but not measured yet is "Next step: set the scale" (still a warning once rooms or receivers are drawn on it), and when there are only next steps the banner says "NOTHING TO FIX". Every check is still there.
- **The status dot's "learning suspended" is said plainly:** "Learning is paused for now: rooms come from the live signals alone". The warning under Scanner Health says "1 receiver often points to a different room than the others" instead of "below 70% reliability".
- **Health → Scanner Health calls its last column "Reliability", what it is.** It said "Weight", but the figure is how often a receiver agrees with the room the others point to, from 0.5 to 1, and nothing is weighted by it. The help says so too, in every language.

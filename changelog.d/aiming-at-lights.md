# 3D look: easier to hit the light you mean

### Changed (the 3D look on the Atlas tab and its wall screens, while "Tap a light to switch it" is on)
- **A light in reach beats a sensor.** With a light and a sensor, a lock, a leak mark or a motion marker both under the finger, the light answers. Before, the nearer of the two did, so a tap meant for a light could open the sensor beside it. With no light in reach, each of those answers as before. A tag or a person under the finger still comes first.
- **A tap that lands on nothing goes to the nearest light within 60 pixels**, if no wall or floor hides it. A fixture a few pixels across no longer has to be hit exactly. Fans are left out.
- **What you press says its name beside your finger** while you hold it. Sliding away before lifting still cancels, as before.
- **A light switched by a tap says so, with Undo**: "Kitchen East off · Undo" for a few seconds. A wrong light costs one more tap.

Not changed: what a tap does on each kind of device (a motion sensor's activity, a lock's switch) stays the same as on the flat Map.

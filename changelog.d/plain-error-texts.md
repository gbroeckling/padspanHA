# Error messages in plain words

### Changed
- **When something can't be done, PadSpan says why in plain words.** About a hundred of the server's refusals named its internal parts: "ModelStore not loaded", "FabricStore not loaded", "Maps store not initialized", "barrier_id is required", "Entity '…' not found in registry". They are sentences now: "PadSpan hasn't finished loading. Try again in a minute; if this keeps happening, restart Home Assistant and look for PadSpan in its log.", "Pick a receiver first.", "Give the phone a name.", "There's no area called Kitchen in Home Assistant." Each keeps its error code, so nothing that reads them changes.
- **Error messages at the top of the panel show those sentences, not raw error text.** Some 175 places showed an error as it came, which could be "[object Object]", a bare "3" for a lost connection, Home Assistant's "required key not provided @ data['map_id']" or a page bug's "Cannot read properties of undefined". PadSpan's own message is shown as it is; for the rest you get a plain line that says what to do ("The connection to Home Assistant was lost. Wait a moment and try again.", "This page and the PadSpan on Home Assistant don't match. Refresh the page; if that doesn't help, restart Home Assistant.").

# Carry-along covers walls, beacons and sub-zones; Align can suggest a fit

### Added
- **A moved plan's signal walls, fixed beacons and sub-zones can move with it.** Review in Mapping → Library now also lists the signal walls drawn in and along the plan's rooms, the fixed beacons standing in them and the sub-zones inside them, each unticked like the rest.
- **"Suggest a fit" in Mapping → 3D Stack.** Works out where a plan sits over the Reference plan's floor from the rooms drawn on both, and shows it for you to check: nothing is saved until you press Save. It needs two or more rooms on the plan and three or more on the other floor, and stays silent rather than guess. One room alone is never fitted this way: use Point Align.

### Changed
- **Carry-along offers only what stood on the plan when it was moved** and has not been touched since. Trimming, turning or extending a plan's picture is no move.

### Usage report (opt-in)
- Which of walls, beacons and sub-zones moved with a plan; a suggested fit shown, or none to suggest. Words only.

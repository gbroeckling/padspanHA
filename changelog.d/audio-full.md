# Audio: full control of a room's sound

### New
- **What's playing, and the buttons to run it.** In a room's card on the Atlas, each player that is playing or paused shows its cover art, the title and the artist, with previous, play/pause and next. A player that is only "on" gets play and pause as two buttons, an idle one just play, and stop shows where a player has no pause. An idle or paused player says so and puts no speaker on the map. Each button appears only if that player supports it.
- **A volume slider** for players that can be set to a level. It answers at once, a vertical swipe still scrolls the card, and Home Assistant gets only a few updates while you drag. Players that step up and down, like Control4 rooms, keep − and + beside it.
- **Source and sound mode.** Pick a player's source from the card: up to four show as buttons, and a longer list opens with a filter box. Sound modes work the same way. A long list, like a Denon receiver's, is grouped into Direct, Stereo, Auto, Movie/music/game, Decoders and DSP, with what the receiver is decoding right now shown beside it. A Control4 room shows no On button, because its On can start a TV. Pick a source to start the room instead.
- **Fast-forward and rewind for Control4 rooms,** once the Control4 integration offers it.
- **Sound ›** opens the tone, EQ and surround settings that the player's integration adds in Home Assistant: bass, treble, balance, loudness, EQ, subwoofer, surround, speech enhancement, dialogue, night mode, lip sync and the receiver's tone, EQ and decoder modes. It covers Sonos, Music Assistant, Yamaha MusicCast, Russound, Xantech and Nuvo, plus Dynamic EQ on a Denon. Only what the player really has is shown.
- **Share ›** plays the same music in other rooms. Tick a room to join it, untick it to let it go, give each room its own volume, or leave the group. This only works between players of the same kind, for example Sonos with Sonos. The room's row says who it's playing with.
- **Any audio in house off** under the players in a room's card while something in the house is playing. It asks first, then turns off every player matched to a room that is playing. One that can't turn off is paused, stopped or muted instead. Paused and idle players are left alone.
- **Tidy up the sources, and cap the volume** (an administrator's job, in Match rooms). For each player you can rename its sources, hide the ones nobody uses and put them in order, and set a volume limit from 10 to 100 %. The limit holds everywhere PadSpan sets a level: the slider stops there, and + greys out once it's reached. Home Assistant keeps its own source names.

### Changed
- **Room off also quiets players that can't turn off.** A playing Sonos is paused (or stopped, or muted) with the rest of the room, and counted in the button.
- **An off player shows only On,** plus its sources where picking one starts it. Mute, volume and Sound come back once it is on.
- **Shades: a row for each shade,** with how far it is open and its own Open, Stop and Close, plus an All row for two or more. Dampers, windows and garage or door openers are never listed as shades; a cover named as a door but also as a blind or shade ("Patio door blind") still is one.
- **A thermostat that holds a range** (heat to and cool to) gets − and + for each, instead of a row with nothing to press. The two are kept at least 1 °C (2 °F) apart, as thermostats that need a gap expect. The room's card no longer repeats the room's temperature under Thermostats or in the list.
- **Match rooms moved to Audio's Set up ›** in the ⋯ list, so a room's card is shorter. It is not offered on a wall screen, where the room's card is also larger.
- **The volume slider keeps its 0 to 100 scale** when a player has a limit: the part past the limit is marked, and the knob stops there.

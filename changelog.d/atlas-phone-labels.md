# A bigger Atlas on a phone; room names you can read

### Changed
- **The Atlas uses a phone's whole width.** On a phone the map sat in a narrow card beside a column of round buttons, so the house was a thumbnail. The buttons are now a row above the map and the map runs edge to edge. Desktops, tablets and wall screens look as they did.

### Fixed
- **Room names on the Atlas stay readable.** A room's name could sit under a strip light, a person's badge or a tag's name ("Larder" under a strip, "Walk-in Closet" under a badge). The name now moves to a clear spot inside its own room, and back once someone walks off it. In a room too full for that, the name is drawn on top. The same on every floor and every Showcase theme.

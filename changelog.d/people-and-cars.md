# People and cars drawn again in Live Aboard

### Changed
- **Cars look like cars.** A car in the 3D house was a slab with a box on top. It now has a real side view with wheel arches cut into it, glass that leans in under a roof, and satin paint in place of shiny plastic. There are two characters:
  - **Sedan and Hatch: a low coupe.** A low nose with round lamps, one sweep of roof down to the tail, five-spoke wheels and a red light bar across the back.
  - **SUV and Pickup: a boxy 4x4.** A flat hood, round lamps at the ends of a dark grille with a bar of light between them, flared arches over big tyres, and a dark top. The SUV carries a spare on the back; the pickup has its bed.
- **People are tokens.** A person was a box with stick arms and legs. A person is now one smooth body in two colours (the top over the bottom) under a head: no arms or legs. Height, build, hair, glasses, cap and colours all still apply, and a child still has a bigger head for its height.
- Nothing needs setting up again. Each car keeps its size, colours, body style and charge-port light, and each person keeps their figure's settings.

# Edgecut: the rooms fill the screen

### New
- **Edgecut.** A new line in the Atlas's ⋯ list (and in the 3D house's Views ▾), off unless a screen ticks it: the map zooms in until the rooms fill the screen edge to edge, with no empty ground beside the house. The house runs off the screen the other way: its outer edges, and on a stack of floors the top and bottom floors, are cut. It works on the flat map and in the 3D house, keeps the 3D camera's angle, and frames again when the screen changes size or another floor is chosen. Drag to another part of the house and it stays there; zoom by hand and your zoom wins until you go back to 100% (the flat map) or the camera's idle return (the 3D house). Each screen keeps its own choice: tick it on the wall and the laptop stays as it was.

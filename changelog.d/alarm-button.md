# Your house alarm inside the emergency button

### New
- **Your house alarm, inside the Atlas's emergency button (PadSpan Bright and Pro).** If Home Assistant has an alarm panel (Alarmo, or your alarm system's own integration), the round emergency button at the top right of the Atlas now holds it. Its centre is the alarm's shield, in the alarm's colour: grey disarmed, blue armed, amber pending, red and pulsing while it goes off. Press the button for its card: the alarm on top, with Arm home, Arm away, Arm night and the rest (only the modes your alarm supports) or Disarm, and your emergency lights below. Every arm and disarm asks first, and asks for the code when the alarm wants one; PadSpan never keeps the code. A wrong code is refused by Home Assistant, and PadSpan says so in Home Assistant's own words. It works on wall screens and for every household member who can use the alarm in Home Assistant, as unlocking a door does.
- **The button changes with what your house has.** Emergency lights and no alarm: the red emergency lighting button exactly as before. An alarm and no emergency lights: the alarm's own button, a plain ring around the shield. Both: the red emergency ring with the alarm's shield as its centre; the lighting test then starts from **Start test** in the card, so a hand going for the alarm never starts it. Neither: no button.
- **An alarm going off shows in the red emergency banner, on every screen and every edition.** The same banner a leak, smoke, gas or CO alarm raises, naming what set it off when the alarm says (Alarmo does): "Alarm going off: House · Back door". On the free edition the button shows the alarm only while it is going off, and its card says so with nothing to press.
- **The opt-in usage report counts it**: the alarm's controls shown, an arm, a disarm, one Home Assistant refused. Counts only, never a code or which alarm.

# Settings in sections

### Changed
- **Settings → Presence is in sections.** Its thirty-odd cards now sit under eight headings: Rooms and timing, Phones and guest tags, What the panel shows, Positioning and calibration, Receivers, Performance, Home Assistant and integrations, and Privacy, updates and feedback. Click a heading to fold it away; this browser remembers which ones you folded. Every section starts open, and no setting, default or card text changed.
- **Quiet mode is switched in one place: the Quiet tick at the top of the Overview.** Its card in Settings still explains it, shows whether it is on, and opens the Overview (the classic Overview when the Atlas is your Overview).
- **"Aggressive BLE reseed (5s)" moved next to the BLE Reseed Interval it shortens**, in Settings → Presence → Receivers. Manage → HA Entities → BLE Proxy Compatibility now says whether it is on and opens that section.

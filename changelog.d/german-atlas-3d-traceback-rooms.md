# Rooms, Traceback, the Atlas and Live Aboard in German

### New
- **Rooms, Traceback, the Atlas and Live Aboard speak German.** With your Home Assistant profile set to German: the Rooms page; Traceback with Playback, New Objects, Insights, Busy Times and the distance table; the Atlas (the map's controls, room and floor sheets, the control card, the door and lock card, emergency lighting, Vacation Mode's options, presets, the light index, the heights list and the scene row); Live Aboard's bar, menus, cards, the Show on this screen list and People & devices; and Edit and Furnish with Strip, From a photo, Import and every piece of furniture's name and settings.
- **The Atlas page loads its own language.** Opened from the sidebar or on a wall screen, it was English whatever your profile said, the Hide HA button included. It now follows your profile like the rest of PadSpan.

### Changed
- French and Dutch stay English on these screens for now.
- Still English in German: Edit → Plans, the shared furniture library, Set up Live Aboard, the power team card, the wall screens card, Showcase theme and Automorph style names, furniture option words (slim, rolled, queen), door type names, and a few single words whose English means two things (the index columns Light and Health, a door's state Open, and Top, Shade, Main, Bath, Build, Share and Tag in Furnish).

# Share a replay

### New
- **Share a replay: a short clip of people moving through your house, ready to post.** In Traceback's playback, pick the stretch (ten minutes to a few hours works best) and press **Share a replay**. PadSpan turns it into a 10 to 30 second clip on the floor stack Traceback plays on. The clip starts where the first person changes room and plays still stretches fast, so it leads with people moving, not with the house.
- **Privacy is the point.** Names become **Person 1**, **Person 2** (or initials); device names, addresses and signal readings are left out; room names stay as they are or become generic ones (**Bedroom**, **Room 3**); floors are numbered, never named; only the time of day shows, never the date. The clip is drawn from your rooms' outlines, so no plan image, address or coordinates are in it. Before you save, the dialog plays the clip as it will be saved and lists what it hides, with each name and room as the clip shows it.
- **Wide or vertical, MP4, WebM or GIF.** MP4 where the browser can record it (Chrome and Edge can), saved as a plain MP4 at a steady 30 frames a second; WebM where it can't (Firefox); a GIF in any browser. A small **made with PadSpan** mark sits in a corner; untick it to leave it out. The clip is made in your browser and saved to your computer: PadSpan uploads nothing. Free in every edition. The 3D house comes later.
- **In the opt-in report:** the dialog opened, a clip saved by format and shape, initials or Person N, room names kept, the mark switched off, a clip that failed. Counts only, never a name, a room or the clip.

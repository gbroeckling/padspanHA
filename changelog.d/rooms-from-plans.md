# Rooms from plans (experimental)

### New
- **Rooms from plans (experimental, Claude Opus 5.5 or newer).** Live Aboard → Edit → Plans gets a third tab beside Line up plans. On a floor whose line-up has ended, Claude reads each lined-up plan twice, the whole plan each time, for its rooms, doors, windows and stairs together. Your rooms are matched by where they lie, never by their printed names, and are never renamed or removed. Where the two reads agree, that outline is proposed even if yours differs; a room whose two reads differ says "look closely" and shows both reads. Printed spaces inside none of your rooms can be merged into a room, added as a new room with a name you pick, or left out; rooms and spaces selected on the map merge into one clean outline. Doors and windows both reads found are placed on their walls; one read only is a maybe, unticked; one already in your house is left. Stairs are matched against the 3D house's.
- **One save for the floor, one undo.** Accept all for this floor saves everything ticked in one save, after a check of your calibration spots (refused if the rooms would place them more than 2 points worse) and one safety backup. Undo this rooms session, in the tab and in Settings → AI, puts it all back; anything changed since is left as it is. Off unless Build from plans is on; signal walls and open edges aren't read.

# Calibration tips that make the walk count

### Changed
- **Two tips wherever you calibrate.** Pin & Listen, Roam, Walk a room and Calibration's Results now say: carry the phone the way you usually do (in a pocket, screen off) and press the buttons on another phone, tablet or computer; and if you moved a receiver, calibrate the rooms next to it again. A phone in your hand with its screen on is heard differently from the same phone in your pocket, which is how PadSpan hears it the rest of the day.
- **Walk a room** no longer suggests holding the phone in your hand while you walk.

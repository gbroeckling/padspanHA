# Drawing rooms on a scanned plan, and what to click when lining plans up

### Added
- **A room's corner lands on the scanned wall end.** On a plan drawn from a room scan, a corner clicked close to a scanned wall end is put exactly on it, so a room traced over a scan matches the scan's own measurements.
- **"Use a scanned outline instead."** Under the drawing buttons on a scanned plan: choose a room, press it, and click inside the scanned room. Its outline becomes that room's, to keep or draw over. Drawing the room yourself stays the first way; this only saves the clicks.
- **Point Align says what to click.** Between plans of two floors it names what runs through both: stairwell corners, an elevator or chimney, a plumbing wall, the house's outside corners.

### Usage report (opt-in)
- A corner landed on a scanned wall end; a scanned outline taken for a room. Words only.

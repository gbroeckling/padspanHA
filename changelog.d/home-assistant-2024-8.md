# Home Assistant 2024.8 or newer

### Changed
- **PadSpan needs Home Assistant 2024.8 or newer.** It said 2024.1, but on Home Assistant before 2024.8 the sensors of the devices it tracks could not be added, so a device had no room sensor. HACS now offers PadSpan only on 2024.8 and newer. If your Home Assistant is older, update Home Assistant first. PadSpan has been checked on Home Assistant releases from 2024.8 to 2026.11.

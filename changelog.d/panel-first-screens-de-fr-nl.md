# The first screens in German, French and Dutch

### New
- **The first screens of the panel are in German, French and Dutch.** When your Home Assistant profile is set to one of them, the screens a new house meets first now speak it: the sample house's banner and the Data switch, Getting started, Setup with its steps, the Setup Wizard's own steps and buttons, Track Your Phone and the phone setup, and the Improve accuracy tips. Home Assistant's own menus are named as Home Assistant names them in your language. The rest of the panel is still English for now and will follow; any other language sees English as before.

### Changed
- **The translated help names these screens as you see them.** Where the German, French or Dutch help mentions a button on the first screens, it now uses the translated word on the button; for the rest of the panel it still quotes the English name.

# "nan" and "inf" are not numbers

### Fixed
- **"nan" and "inf" are refused where a number is expected.** A light's size and turn, a receiver's height and its signal offset, and the end of a guest's stay took the words "nan" and "inf" as numbers and stored them, where they spoil every position worked out from them and are saved as nothing at all. They are refused now, with the rest of what is not a number. A guest's stay changed later can no longer end further ahead than one started now can (a year).

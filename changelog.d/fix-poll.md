# Quiet devices keep their room, arrivals need a device to be heard, receivers judged fairly

### Fixed
- **A device that has gone quiet stays in the room it was in.** A device that left the house, or was switched off, could be shown moving to another room about half a minute later, sometimes on another floor, with a room-change alert to match. Nothing had heard it move: the receivers that heard it best had let go of their readings, and an old reading from a receiver that seldom heard it was all that was left. With nothing new from any receiver for 20 seconds, a device now keeps its room until it is heard again.
- **No "arrived" for a device nobody heard.** When PadSpan stopped listing a device that had been silent for a long time (at the BLE Advertisement Timeout, or when its history ran out), it was counted as arriving: the arrive alert, `padspan_device_arrived` and arrive rules ran hours or days after it had left, and its device tracker read its old room for two minutes instead of `not_home`. An arrival now needs the device to be heard. A device that drops off the list while it still counts as home is now reported as departed when it is dropped; before, it never was.
- **Receivers are no longer marked unreliable for hearing devices in other rooms.** A receiver was marked wrong each time it heard a device that was in another room, which is most of what any receiver hears. In a house with devices in several rooms every receiver drifted to about 50% reliability: System Critics listed them as "disagrees with consensus", and their readings were weakened by up to 5 dB whenever a room was picked by strongest signal. A receiver is now judged only on the devices it hears loudest. After this update most receivers should read at or near 100%; one that stays low is often the loudest for devices PadSpan places in another room, so check its area and where it sits.
- **A pinned beacon reports its room once.** A pinned beacon is shown in its pin's room. When its solved position fell in the room next door, it sent a room-change alert, a movement-history entry and a tag event again and again while the room it was shown in never changed. It now reports a room change only when the room it is shown in changes.

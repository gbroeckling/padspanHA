# Quiet devices keep their room, arrivals need a device to be heard

### Fixed
- **A device that has gone quiet stays in the room it was in.** A device that left the house, or was switched off, could be shown moving to another room about half a minute later, sometimes on another floor, with a room-change alert to match. Nothing had heard it move: the receivers that heard it best had let go of their readings, and an old reading from a receiver that seldom heard it was all that was left. With nothing new from any receiver for about 15 seconds (three polls at the default 5 s), a device now keeps its room until it is heard again.
- **No "arrived" for a device nobody heard.** When PadSpan stopped listing a device that had been silent for a long time (at the BLE Advertisement Timeout, or when its history ran out), it was counted as arriving: the arrive alert, `padspan_device_arrived` and arrive rules ran hours or days after it had left, and its device tracker read its old room for two minutes instead of `not_home`. An arrival now needs the device to be heard. A device that drops off the list while it still counts as home is now reported as departed when it is dropped; before, it never was.
- **A pinned beacon reports its room once.** A pinned beacon is shown in its pin's room. When its solved position fell in the room next door, it sent a room-change alert, a movement-history entry and a tag event again and again while the room it was shown in never changed. It now reports a room change only when the room it is shown in changes.
- **A room-change event or alert is not sent twice running.** `padspan_room_change` events (and the tag scans with them) and Follow's room alerts are spaced at least 30 and 60 seconds apart. A device that changed room, went back within that time and changed again was reported as making the same change twice, with nothing between; and after PadSpan had lost sight of a device for a minute, its next event could start in a room the last event had not ended in. Each event and alert now starts in the room the last one ended in, and a device back in the room last reported sends nothing. A device not listed for a minute or two and then heard in another room is reported as moving from the room it was shown in, where it was reported as placed from nowhere.

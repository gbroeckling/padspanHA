# The add-ons, sorted out: free shades, Climate & air quality, Presence insights with Forensics

### Changed
- **Shades are free.** A room's blinds and shades are in its card on the Atlas, with how far open they are and Open, Stop and Close for them all, on any install with no key. Tick **Shades** in a screen's ⋯ list, under "Doors, windows and locks", or turn on **Settings → Atlas → Shades** on the classic layout. They used to come with Audio. Garage doors, gates and any cover linked as a door's opener never show up under Shades: they stay in their own door card, part of Alarm & security.
- **Thermostats moved to the climate add-on, now called Climate & air quality.** Each thermostat in a room's card, with its mode and setpoint, and the room's temperature and humidity at the top of the card, are now part of the add-on that has the ♨ Heatmap. It was called Climate map, and the price is the same. Tick **Thermostats** in the ⋯ list, under "Motion and sensors", or turn on **Settings → Atlas → Thermostats**.
- **Audio** is now each room's players, Room off, the speaker beside a room's name and Match rooms.
- **A screen that had Audio on keeps its shades and thermostats.** Their new lines, or the Settings switches on the classic layout, start ticked there, so nothing disappears from a room's card after the update.
- **Forensics is part of Presence insights.** One add-on now has Locate, Busy Times and Forensics, at the same price. A key that already holds Forensics gets all of Presence insights.
- **A light's size and angle come with Advanced smart lighting,** alongside its fixture shape. Use Transform, or Width, Length and Rotate in the builder. They used to need PadSpan Pro.
- **A wall screen goes by its own ⋯ list for Audio** too, the same way its map's speakers already did. The classic layout's Settings switch stays for the house.
- **The add-on sale says "through January 1"**, which is the last day it runs.

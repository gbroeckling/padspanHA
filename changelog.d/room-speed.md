# Room changes in seconds, not a minute

### Changed
- **A room change shows in seconds instead of over a minute.** Walk from the hall into the bedroom and PadSpan now shows the bedroom about 12 seconds later where the room's scanner hears well, and about 25 seconds later where it hears only now and then (a proxy left at ESPHome's standard scan settings catches about one advert in ten). With the house drawn on the map it takes a few seconds more. Before, a move took a minute and a half (median), and a short visit to a room was often never shown at all. Measured on simulated walks through PadSpan's own code, as no recorded walk with marked rooms exists yet. A device standing still switches rooms by mistake no more often than before, and far less often with the house drawn.
- **Every scanner is read on every update.** Home Assistant passes only the strongest scanner's adverts along as they arrive, so the others' readings were refreshed every 30 seconds and could be half a minute old. PadSpan now reads every scanner's list every 5 seconds, skipping adverts it already has, so it costs little more than before. Settings → Presence → Bluetooth refresh interval now defaults to 5 seconds.
- **Signal smoothing follows a real move at once.** When one scanner suddenly hears a device much more strongly, the device has moved, and PadSpan follows it straight away (and drops the readings of scanners that have heard nothing since). A reading that is suddenly much weaker on its own is usually a passing fade and only counts a little. The smoothing setting (Q) now defaults to 3, and the panel accepts up to 10; the old 0.125 was meant for smoothing each advert, many a second, not one update every 5 seconds.
- **A clear move next door needs half the Room Change Delay.** When the new room's scanner is well ahead of the old room's for two updates in a row, the device moves then. A close call, as by a doorway, still waits the whole Room Change Delay, and so do far-apart rooms, another floor and going outdoors.
- **Installs on the old defaults move to the new ones once.** If you never changed the smoothing (0.125) or the Bluetooth refresh interval (30 s), they become 3 and 5 s. A value you set yourself is kept.

### Fixed
- **A scanner that hears a device only now and then no longer moves it next door.** Twenty seconds without a new advert counted as the device having gone, even from a scanner that usually goes that long between adverts; the room's own scanner then faded and a still device was shown in the next room. Silence is now judged against that scanner's own gaps.
- **With the house drawn, a still device flickers between rooms far less.** Its worked-out position wanders a metre or so with the noise; it now has to be clearly inside the next room before it counts for it, as the strongest-scanner method already needed a 3 dB lead.
- **Each advert counts once.** A scanner's last reading was used again on every update while it was under 15 seconds old, so an old reading pulled harder than one reading should.
- **Replay & Score measures time in the recording's seconds.** The vote's "just arrived" rules read the clock while the replay ran, so in a replay every move looked like it came seconds after the last.

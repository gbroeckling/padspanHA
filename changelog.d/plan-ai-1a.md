# Add data via plans: a first, read-only look (experimental)

### New
- **AI map-building research switch (experimental, off).** One switch in Settings → AI now covers every AI feature that builds your map: the stair finder you may already use, and the new Plans tool below. Off hides them all. A home that had the stair finder turned on when it updated comes up with the new switch on, so nobody loses it; every other home has it off. It is an administrator's setting, like the AI Task itself.
- **Add data via plans, in Live Aboard's Edit bar (Plans).** With the switch on and an AI Task chosen, Edit gets a Plans button. Tick the floor plans you placed and your AI reads them for staircases, looks again at a crop round each one to place it (a find that closer look doesn't confirm is left out), and asks whether a handrail is drawn beside it, all inside an 8-minute budget on the server (the page can close and come back). Before anything is sent, the sheet says where the plans go: a local AI keeps them in the house; a cloud AI needs a tick, a backup AI Task reads them only with a tick of its own, and a plan with your address on it goes with it. Each find is matched against the stairs already in your 3D house (already there, a correction, new, or a second staircase stacked over one) and listed with a crop of the plan for you to check item by item. **This version writes nothing to your house:** you can mark an item wrong (with a reason) or later, and manage the items you marked wrong, which are remembered for that plan until it is moved or replaced. Keeping an item as new or as a correction comes in the next beta. Lights, doors and windows are not read.
- **Usage report (when on):** counts only for the new tool: how a session ended, whether the AI was local, how many plans and reads, how long each read took, each failure by reason, what was suggested and what was already there, each verdict and its reason. Never a plan, an answer, a name or a position.

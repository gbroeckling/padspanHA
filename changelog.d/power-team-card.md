# Power teams on the map: one card for the wall dimmer and its bulbs, a wake-up, and the day's curve in the card

### New
- **A power team is one thing on the map.** Place the wall dimmer, one of its bulbs or the team's own light: a tap switches the team, a drag dims it, and a hold opens the team's card. A dimmer left on with its bulbs off shows dark, as the room is.
- **The team's card.** It says what the team is doing (off with the power on or cut, starting its bulbs, on but a bulb not answering, following the day, sleep, set by hand, waking up), with Follow the day and Sleep, the wall dimmer's power (on, left low at the wall, off or out of reach, with Cut power and Power on), and each bulb with its light, and Try again for one that did not answer. It updates while it is open.
- **Off can leave the power on.** Each team chooses what off does: turn the bulbs off and leave the power on, so they come back at once in the right colour, or cut the power as before. Teams you already have keep cutting the power; new teams leave it on. Change it in the card's Advanced section or in Settings → Atlas → Power teams.
- **A wake-up light.** On the days and at the time you choose, a team that is off comes on at its dimmest and warmest and fades to full light by then. Lights already on are left alone. Set it in the team's card.
- **The day's curve in the card,** with when this light's day starts and ends: the sun's sunrise and sunset moved by minutes, or fixed times (a bedroom whose day starts at 6:30 all year). Adapted from Adaptive Lighting, as the curve is.
- **Fades of any length.** A team's light now takes a transition, up to 100 minutes, so an automation can fade it in slowly; while it fades the day does not move it.

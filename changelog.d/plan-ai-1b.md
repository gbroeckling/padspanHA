# Add data via plans: keep what the AI found, undo it in one go (experimental)

### New
- **Keep a stair the AI found, or take its position (Plans tool, experimental).** In Live Aboard's Edit → Plans list, New and Take the AI's position now work. They go into your draft like any other edit, so Undo and Discard take them back and nothing is kept until you press Save. A new stair is labelled "Stairs (from the plan)" and has no railing. A second staircase stacked over one asks first which floor it climbs from. A correction moves only where the stair stands, never its shape or turn, and on a stair you placed yourself the keyboard starts on Keep mine, which notes "already here" and leaves your stair alone. A railing hint asks for a side and a style and goes on a stair that is kept. There is no "accept all".
- **Save checks first, and writes only your 3D house.** Save sends what you kept through one command: it checks each item again and refuses any whose stair or plan changed since you looked ("changed since you looked"; a stair you deleted never comes back), takes one safety backup per session, and writes your 3D house and its own record together. Map, rooms, walls, floors, scale, settings and calibration are never written. If a write fails, or Home Assistant stops between the two, everything is as it was or the whole keep stands, never half. Two admins, or a double click, make one stair. Your other edits in the same Save go after it, as they always do.
- **Undo this session, in the list and in Settings → AI.** One undo takes back everything a session kept, exactly as it was. Anything you changed since is left as it is, and it says how many. The undo can itself be put back. Marks of wrong and later stay as they are: they go to the server the moment you click them, so neither Discard nor a closed page loses them.
- **Usage report (when on):** counts only: each item kept as new or as a correction, per kind; corrections taken on a stair placed by hand or by the AI; a kept stair moved over 30 cm before Save; a stacked stair taken; whose side a railing kept has; how each keep ended; each undo, by kind, and what it left. Never a plan, a name or a position.

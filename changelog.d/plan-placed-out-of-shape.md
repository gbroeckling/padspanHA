# A floor plan placed out of shape is said

### Added
- **Overview's "Improve accuracy" card names a floor plan placed wider or narrower than its own picture.** A plan dragged to size in the old 3D Stack could end up squeezed: a nearly square picture standing 31% too narrow, with its room and receivers up to a metre off, and nothing said so. Now a plan whose placed shape is more than 12% from its picture's is named, with how far and which way, and a Measure button that opens the scale step on that plan. A plan with a hand measurement of its own is trusted and never named; a plan with nothing drawn on it is left alone. Like the other tips, it shows to administrators only, never on a wall screen, and can be hidden.

### Usage report (opt-in)
- **How many plans are placed out of shape**, as a count, and whether the new tip was shown, followed or hidden. Never a plan's name or picture.

# Voice questions shown in the panel

### New
- **"Which room is Alice in?" is explained where you set PadSpan up.** Home Assistant's Assist can answer which room someone is in, but only once its sentences are added to Home Assistant, and those were only in the online guide. Settings → Tracking → Home Assistant and integrations now has "Ask Assist which room someone is in": the questions as examples, in the name of one of your own devices, the answers it gives, and the sentences to save as `config/custom_sentences/en/padspan.yaml` with a "Copy the sentences" button.

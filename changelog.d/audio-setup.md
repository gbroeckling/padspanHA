# Audio: its own name, and its setup in the ⋯ list

### Changed
- **Room controls are called Audio.** The line in the Atlas map's ⋯ (Show on this screen) now says **Audio**, in the flat map's list and the 3D view's. What it does is the same, and a screen that had it ticked keeps its tick.

### New
- **Set up › on the Audio line.** The ⋯ list's **Audio** line has a **Set up ›** that opens the matching card (which player is in which room, **Match Control4 to HA** in a Control4 house) right there over the map, ticked or not, in the flat map's list and the 3D view's, on a wall screen too. It is an administrator's, as the matching always was.
- **The speaker by a room's name, in 3D too.** With **Audio** ticked, the 3D view now shows the same small speaker the flat map does at a room's name while one of its players is on, struck through while the room's first player is muted, and nothing while they are all off. Where the names are hidden it stands where the name would be. A tap on it opens the room's panel, with its Audio; a tap on the map never switches anything.

### Fixed
- **A player's buttons stay on one line.** In the 3D view's room panel on a wall screen, a player's **Mute**, **−**, level, **+** and ⏻ wrapped onto two lines. They now stay together on one line, under the player's name where the panel is narrow.

# Plans from Easy Floorplan, DXF and Floorplanner

### New
- **Import a plan from another app.** Mapping → Upload → **No floor plan? Ways to get one** has a new way, second on the list: pick an Easy Floorplan card's YAML, a DXF drawing (saved as text) or a Floorplanner export (the `.fml`, or the zip it comes in), and PadSpan draws that floor to scale, the way it draws a phone's room scan. Easy Floorplan brings its walls, doors, windows and rooms, and a room linked to a Home Assistant area is named after it; a DXF's closed polylines become rooms, named by the text inside them, with the unit from the drawing; Floorplanner brings its walls, doors, windows and rooms in centimetres. A file with no unit asks for the drawing's width (an Easy Floorplan drawing without one is scaled from its doors, and says so), and a file with several floors asks which one. The file's rooms are outlines, not new rooms: choose each Home Assistant room and press **Use the outline the file gives** it. Whatever isn't used (devices and their entities, furniture, texts, curves drawn straight) is listed, in the message and beside the rooms on the plan. Free, like every way to a plan.

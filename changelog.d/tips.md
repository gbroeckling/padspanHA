# Tips about what your house already has

### New
- **A tip now and then about a feature you already have and don't use yet.** At most one a day, and none in an install's first week, so PadSpan first sees what you use. It picks what fits your house: its lights, sensors, players, receivers and floor plans, and what your licence includes, so it never tries to sell you anything. A tip you've seen, dismissed or already use never comes back. **Show me** opens the exact screen where the feature is turned on, **Not now** puts it away, and **No more tips** turns them all off.
- **Where tips show.** On the Overview, as a quiet card in the one spot other cards use, only when no other card wants it. On the Atlas, only while you're adjusting it: in a screen's ⋯ list while it's open, in **Settings → Atlas** and in **Mapping → Atlas builder**. Never on the map itself, in a room's card, or on a wall screen. A tip that needs an administrator shows only to administrators, and in Basic mode only tips for screens Basic has show up.
- **Settings → Appearance → Tips** turns tips on or off (they're on unless you turn them off) and lists every tip that fits your house, including ones you've already seen, each with Show me.
- **The order gets better with the opt-in usage report.** When two tips fit your house equally well, the one other homes use most comes first. The daily update check brings the ranking, and your install sends nothing extra to get it. With the usage report on, PadSpan counts each tip shown, followed, put off or dismissed, tips turned off, and whether the feature was used within a week of its tip. These are counts only.
- **In German**, like the Atlas.

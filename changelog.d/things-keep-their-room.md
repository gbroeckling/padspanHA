# Keys can keep their room

### New
- **`last_room` on the area sensor and the device tracker:** the room the device was last seen in. It stays there after the device reads not_home, so an automation or a card can still say where it was left.
- **An option: keys, tags and other things can stay in their room for hours.** It is off unless you turn it on, and nothing changes until you do. Every device switches to not_home once it has not been heard for the away timeout (5 minutes unless you changed it), so keys in a drawer or a tag on a bag go "away" while they sit in the kitchen. If you would rather they stayed in their room, set a number of hours in Settings → Presence → Home/Away Timeout, under "Things keep their room": 0.5 to 48 hours, or 0 for off. What a person carries still goes away after the away timeout; anything nobody carries keeps the room it was last seen in for the hours you set, then switches to not_home. This is the area sensor and the device tracker in Home Assistant; the PadSpan panel, arrive and leave alerts and events are unchanged.
- **Before you turn it on: what counts as carried.** A device is carried when it is added to a person in Home Assistant (Settings → People → Track devices), is picked for them in Live Aboard (Furnish → People & devices), or has the person's name. Every other named device counts as a thing. A phone or watch that is not linked to a person would read as home in its last room for those hours after it has left, so add each one to its person first. A car's tag does the same: it stays in the garage for those hours after the car has gone.

# Setup offers the quick room walk first

### New
- **Setup's Calibrate step offers "Walk a room" first where it is switched on.** Its Start opens Calibration's Walk a room tab (walk each room for 90 seconds with your phone), and a "Point by point" button beside it opens Guided calibration as before. Where Walk a room is off (it still is by default), Start opens Guided calibration as it always did, and one line says how to turn the walk on, with "Open that setting" going straight to its switch (just that card in Basic mode).

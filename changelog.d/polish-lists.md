# Lists and tables that fit a phone, and ages you can read

### Fixed
- **Occupancy on a phone: room names are whole again.** In "Rooms with evidence" the room's name was squeezed into a sliver and broke letter by letter. On a narrow screen each room's name now has a line of its own, with its people, phones, sensors and agreement under it. On a desktop the table is as it was.
- **Manage on a phone: the Delete button of every area is on the screen.** The areas table was wider than the screen. A long area ID now wraps onto a second line instead of pushing Delete off the edge.
- **Manage: area IDs are readable.** They were tiny dark grey text on a near-black card; they are now the same size and grey as the other IDs on that page.
- **Manage: the cards on the Data tab no longer touch,** and the help button sits beside the Danger Zone heading instead of alone on a line under it.
- **Devices: "last heard" reads as time, not a count of seconds.** A device heard 56 days ago said "4837956s ago"; it now says "56 days ago" (or seconds, minutes or hours for something more recent).
- **Devices: Details, Rename and Untag stay side by side.** When a row ran out of room the three buttons broke apart differently on every row. They now move to the next line together, on a phone as well.
- **Buttons that looked like text boxes are button sized.** "Find" on Presence, "Search window" on Forensics, "Save & Refresh" and "Save" on Occupancy, and the list buttons under Rooms, Objects and Bluetooth radios on the Overview stretched across their whole card.

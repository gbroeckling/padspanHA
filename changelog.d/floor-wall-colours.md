# Floors and walls in Furnish

### New
- **Floors and wall colours for each room (Live Aboard, Furnish).** In Mapping → Furnish, tap a room's floor: pick its floor (wood, light wood, tile, stone, carpet or concrete, each drawn with its own colour and planks or joints), the colour of its walls inside, one wall in a colour of its own, and the colour of the floor's outside walls. Each is one Undo and kept at Save, in Live Aboard's own file, so a PadSpan backup carries it and a restore brings it back. They show in the Classic and Studio looks; a Showcase theme and the Atlas's look keep their own colours. Nothing changes until you pick something. With the usage report on, it counts which floor material was picked and that walls were coloured, never which room or colour.

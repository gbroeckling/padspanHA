# Line up plans (experimental)

### New
- **Build from plans: advanced plan alignment (Claude, experimental).** A new switch under AI map-building research in Settings → AI, off for everyone. It shows only when your main AI Task is Home Assistant's Anthropic integration running Claude Opus 5.5 or newer (any Fable or Mythos too); any other AI keeps it hidden. Administrators only, Pro, with Live Aboard on.
- **Line up the plans of one floor (experimental, read only in this version).** Pick a floor; Claude reads each plan's printed title, rooms, stairs, outside corners and dimension lines, and PadSpan measures: the ticks of each dimension, pixels per metre, how far a scan is turned, which plan agrees with its own printed dimensions (the one the others are lined up to), and how each other plan matches it picture to picture. A placement is proposed only when the picture match and the printed dimensions agree. A plan that shows another floor is offered a refile. Each proposal says what it would shift: calibration clicks, scanners and lights drawn on the plan, traced rooms. Nothing is moved; saving a line-up comes in a later version, and the Save button says so.
- **The little help.** When something can't be settled it asks one small thing, at most three a floor: confirm a printed dimension, or tap the same corner on two plans. An answer reruns the measuring only, with no new AI read.
- **Every stop says why.** A stop (Claude out of credit or unreachable, out of time, a question waiting, a refile refused because things are drawn on the plan, and the rest) is shown in plain words with what to do next, and Resume where the run can continue. Stops are counted per month on this Home Assistant. It never falls back to another AI.
- **In the usage report, if you have it on:** counts and bands only (runs, reads, stops by reason, fit quality, questions asked and answered, proposals shown and verdicts). Never a plan, a title, a room, a floor, the AI Task, a coordinate or an amount.

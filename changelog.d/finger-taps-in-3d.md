# Finger taps on lights in the 3D look

### Fixed
- **A tap with a finger switches the light in the Atlas's 3D look.** A finger rolls a little as it lands and lifts. If it moved more than 8 pixels (about 3 mm on a wall screen) the tap was treated as the start of a turn of the house and did nothing at all. A finger may now move up to 16 pixels and still count as a tap. A mouse is unchanged, and so is the flat Map.

# Fit keeps Edgecut

### Fixed
- **Fit and 100% no longer forget Edgecut.** With Edgecut ticked, moving the map and pressing Fit (or 100%) went back to the whole house as if Edgecut were off. Now it goes back to Edgecut's own framing, centred. In the 3D house, Iso, Top and ⟳ 90° keep Edgecut on too and frame it from the new angle; only Whole house or a saved view turns it off.

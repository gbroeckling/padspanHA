# Setup: your phone or watch is a step

### New
- **"Track your phone or watch" is now one of Setup's numbered steps**, after the receivers and before calibrating (which you walk with that phone). Its Start opens the phone setup wizard you already had (Bluetooth → IRK Manager → Track your phone); in Basic mode just that card, with the bar back to Setup over it. The step is done once PadSpan follows at least one phone or watch as yours: a phone or watch with its key (IRK) registered, or a Companion-app phone you pressed Track for. Getting started and the Setup Wizard's "Next" lines show the same step, numbered the same. An install that has the phone wizard switched off goes to the Overview's Track Your Phone card instead, opened.
- **The wizard's Android path ends with the one thing it was missing:** press Track for the phone on the Overview's Track Your Phone card, with a button that takes you there.

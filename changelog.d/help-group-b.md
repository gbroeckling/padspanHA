# Help pages brought up to date (Mapping and the Atlas)

### Changed
- **Mapping's help matches the screens again.** Library, Upload, Edit and 3D Stack were rewritten against what the tabs do today: rooms come from the Choose room… list, walls and heights save at once while rooms and receivers wait for Save Layout, the scale takes two measurements, and the Floor Heights card and tie-ins are covered. The Setup Wizard, Layout and Export get their own **?**. The Help tab's manual and Training's floor plan walkthroughs were corrected too: a receiver's room is set in Edit, not in Bluetooth → Scanners.
- **The Atlas has help where it had none.** New pages cover the sidebar Atlas, Vacation Mode (Last week or Monthly average, and every option in its box), wall screens and Live Aboard. Vacation Mode's options box, the wall screens card and the Map | Live Aboard switch get a **?**, and the build controls page now lists Theme, Fit room, Light spread, Scene, Ripple, presets and the layout switch.
- **Reading photos points to the right place.** When no AI Task is set up, the message now sends you to Settings → AI, where the AI Task is chosen.

# Lights stay where you placed them; taps on sensors and locks; darker paving

### Changed
- **3D look: a strip, valance, wall light or panel stays where you placed it unless a wall is within 0.75 m.** Before, each went to a wall of its room however far away, so a light placed over an island was drawn on a wall metres off and a press on what you saw missed it. Within 0.75 m it still goes onto the wall. A strip with a length takes only a wall running its way, so it is never turned. A wall light with no wall in reach is drawn at its spot at its usual height.
- **A tap on a sensor says what it reads**: "Hall temperature: 21.4 °C" at the bottom of the screen, in the flat Map and the 3D look. Before, it said only that sensors are read-only.
- **A tap on a motion sensor says its reading; a long press opens its activity.** Before, any tap opened the full-screen activity history. Both looks.
- **A tap never turns a lock.** A tap or a hold on a lock opens its card, which has Lock and Unlock. Before, a tap locked or unlocked it at once. Both looks.
- **Paving tiles are darker and textured**: a darker grey with darker joints and flecks on each tile, in the flat Map and the 3D look.

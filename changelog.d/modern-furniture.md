# Modern furniture; kitchens, bathrooms and one-tap room sets

### New
- **Kitchen furniture.** Build ▾ → Kitchen: base cabinets (doors, drawers, or a drawer over doors, each with its own worktop, so a row of them reads as one), wall cabinets (doors, glass, lift-up or open), tall cabinets (doors, a built-in oven, or an oven and a microwave), a sink with one or two bowls, a hob in the worktop or a freestanding cooker (induction or gas), a dishwasher and a fridge (tall, double or under the counter). A sink's height goes up to the top of its tap: a 1.2 m sink has its worktop at 0.9 m, level with the cabinets beside it.
- **Bathroom furniture.** Build ▾ → Bathroom: a toilet (on the floor, or hung on the wall), a basin (on a vanity unit, a pedestal or the wall; one or two), a bath (built-in or freestanding) and a shower (a glass enclosure, or a walk-in screen).
- **Room sets: furnish a room in one tap.** Build ▾ → Room sets puts a whole set in the room in the middle of the view: Kitchen, one wall; Kitchen, L-shaped; Bathroom; Bedroom; Living room; Dining room; Office. Pieces go along the walls with their backs against them, step past doors, windows and anything already there, and always stay inside the room. Anything that has no room is left out, and the message says how many. One Undo takes the whole set away. Nothing is kept until you Save.
- **15 new starter pieces** in the Library for the kitchen and the bathroom.

### Changed
- **Furniture looks more modern.** Every piece has been redrawn in one cleaner style: lower and slimmer, with thin tapered legs, cabinets on a dark plinth set back so they seem to float, thinner tops with softened edges, and flush fronts with slim handles. Sofas have plump back cushions, the table lamp has a ceramic base, plants have real leaves, and the wood grain is finer. Your furniture keeps its own sizes, settings and colours; only the drawing is new. Cars and people are not changed.
- **New pieces start in calmer colours**: a grey sofa, light bedding, an oak table and chairs.

# Each problem goes where it is fixed

### New
- **A button on each problem that opens the screen that fixes it.** In Health's "Problems found", a floor plan with no scale has "Measure" (the scale step on that plan), rooms that get mixed up or old calibration have "Calibrate" (the guided calibration), rooms that don't fit their plan have "Open Layout" (Mapping → Layout on that floor), a receiver that often points to the wrong room has "Open Receivers", and learning switched off has "Open the setting" (in Basic, just that setting). A screen your mode doesn't have gets no button.

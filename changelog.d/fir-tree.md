# A fir for the plant, so a Christmas tree looks like one

### New
- **A plant can be a fir.** In Live Aboard's Build, Plant now offers "fir" beside bush, tree, palm and cactus: tiers of green that narrow to a point, on a short trunk, with or without a pot. It is the shape for a Christmas tree; a bush stretched to that height ended in a dark dome. A plant already in your house is unchanged.

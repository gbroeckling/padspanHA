# Every website guide in German too

### New
- **Every website guide now has a German version.** The last three are in German now: connecting Claude to Home Assistant for Let Claude draw your rooms (padspan.traks.ca/guides/claude-ai-task/de/), the whole house on one wall in a large home (/guides/large-homes/de/) and which touch screen to hang for a PadSpan wall (/guides/wall-screens/de/). Each English page links its German one, and the guides list and the sitemap carry them.

### Fixed
- **The wall screen guide no longer tells portrait walls to choose Size for my room.** Since 0.38.181, Size from the screen draws a 4K wall at twice its 1080p size either way up, and the guide now says so, and that a TV which can't turn its picture can hang on its side with Screen orientation in Settings → Atlas → Wall screens. The large-homes guide said the same of landscape screens only.

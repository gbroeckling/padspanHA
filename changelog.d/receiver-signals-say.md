# Signals say this receiver is in another room

### New
- **Setup's receivers step suggests a receiver's room from what it hears.** Once you have calibration points, a receiver whose strongest-heard points are mostly in another room than the one it is filed under gets a line such as "Signals say Kitchen proxy is in Hall, not Kitchen: 3 of the 5 calibration points it hears best are there", with "Use Hall" (one tap sets that Home Assistant area for it) and "Keep Kitchen". It only speaks up when its own room has calibration points too, so a room you haven't calibrated yet never looks like the wrong one.

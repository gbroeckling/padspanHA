# The guides and the help say what PadSpan does today

### Fixed
- **The first-steps guides describe today's first run.** Getting Started still showed the February flow: a five-step wizard, a Sample mode switch, an Objects tab and dragging scanners on a map. It now follows what a new install does: it opens on your own house as soon as a scanner is heard, shows Who's where now, and Make it a map opens Setup, ending with Put it on the map. The website's first-steps guide (English and German) is brought up to 0.38.274, and the Floor Plan Setup page is rewritten: there is no master map, scanners are added from Live BLE Receivers, and nothing reaches the map of your house until Put it on the map.
- **The right tab everywhere.** The website, its guides in English and German, the wall screen guide and the last release notes still sent people to the Settings tab called Presence, which has been Tracking since 0.38.202. Each now names Tracking and the section the setting is in, such as Tracking → Rooms and timing → Home/Away Timeout for "Things keep their room". Wall screens are set in Settings → Atlas, the rooms editor is Mapping → Layout, the lights tool is Mapping → Atlas builder, and Floor Heights is on Mapping → 3D Stack.
- **The in-panel help had three wrong defaults, now right in English, German, French and Dutch:** Signal smoothing's Q is 3.0 with a range of 0.01 to 10 (it said 0.125, up to 1.0, from before November), the Bluetooth refresh interval is 5 seconds (it said 30), and Enhanced security is on for a new install (it said off). Home Assistant's menu is named "Areas, labels & zones", as it is.
- **An iPhone's key added in Home Assistant's Private BLE Device is picked up within five minutes**, not one; one added through PadSpan itself works straight away.
- **No more pointers to things that are gone or never were:** the HACS sign-in note (fixed upstream in October), three experimental features removed in August, home/away binary sensors (home or away is each device's tracker), Wi-Fi in occupancy, and the old WebSocket "API reference", now a short page on the entities, services and events to use instead. Troubleshooting links a page that helps, and Home Assistant 2024.8 is given as the minimum on the requirements page too.
- **The website's Pro list is complete** (placement on the Atlas, Live Aboard with Furnish, wall mode, the Atlas as Overview and its Heatmap, presets and Vacation Mode, Busy Times, Locate, Forensics and four themes), and its comparison with Bermuda and ESPresense is the README's, checked against their own docs.
- **A test keeps it that way:** it fails when a guide, the website, the help or a release note names a Settings tab, a part of a tab or a Mapping tab the panel doesn't have, or another Home Assistant minimum than the one HACS enforces.

# A motion sensor's reach in Live Aboard is soft light

### Changed
- **A motion sensor's reach is drawn as soft light.** With Motion picked, on a trigger, or on hover, Live Aboard used to draw it as a pale plate with a bright outline. That outline cut across rooms, and a sensor outside the house showed a large disc on the ground. It's now a soft pool of light, strongest at the sensor and fading out at its reach. Everything shows where it did.

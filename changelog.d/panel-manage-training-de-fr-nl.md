# Manage and Training in German, French and Dutch

### New
- **Manage speaks German, French and Dutch.** When your Home Assistant profile is set to one of them, every Manage tab is in your language: Data (the danger zone, orphan room shapes, BLE device names, phantom entries, Home Assistant entities, areas and maps, Backup & Restore, integration controls), HA Entities (the entity reference and library, publishing controls, the live inventory, MQTT, Tasmota and ESPresense), Beacon Characteristics, History, Events and the email alerts, Logs and Factory Reset. Entity IDs, attribute names and the YAML examples stay as Home Assistant reads them, and Factory Reset still asks you to type FACTORY RESET.
- **Training speaks German, French and Dutch too:** the hub, every walkthrough with its animated diagrams, the Quick Start and the Full Manual. The note that some of Training was still in English is gone.

### Fixed
- Factory Reset's final warning showed "\n" as text instead of starting new lines.

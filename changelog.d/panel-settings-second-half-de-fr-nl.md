# The rest of Settings in German, French and Dutch

### New
- **All of Settings speaks German, French and Dutch.** When your Home Assistant profile is set to one of them, Features (the PadSpan licence card, its homes list, the tiers tour, the experimental features and Forensics), AI (your AI Task, its usage line and the map-building research switches), Atlas (outdoor weather, Live Aboard's settings, what it flies to, scenes and the Atlas as Overview) and UI Structure are in your language.
- Live Aboard's own buttons and menus are still in English, so Settings names them in English too ("Views ▾ → Show people", "Line up plans").
- The Atlas and the 3D view are still in English; they come next.

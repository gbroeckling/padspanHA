# Walk through the 3D house

### New
- **Walk through your house in Live Aboard.** **Walk** (bottom right; on a narrow screen in **View ▾**) puts you in the house at eye height, just inside the front door or in the room you have open. Walk with **W A S D** or **↑ ↓** (**← →** turn, **Shift** to hurry), or with the stick that shows on a touch screen; drag to look round. Walls, railings, window sills and closed doors stop you, a door opens when its sensor says so, and stairs take you to the floor above and back down. **✕ Stop walking** or **Esc** puts the 3D view back exactly as it was. Standing still, nothing is drawn, so a slow tablet stays cool. Live Aboard is still experimental: tell us where it lets you through a wall or stops you on a stair.
- **The usage report counts walks (only if you send it).** A walk started, and whether it used the keys, the stick, a drag or the stairs. Never where, which room or how far.

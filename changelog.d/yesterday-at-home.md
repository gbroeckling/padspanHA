# Yesterday at home

### New
- **Yesterday at home: one short message each morning about the day before.** Off until you turn it on in Settings → Tracking → Phones and guest tags. At the time you choose it tells, through a Home Assistant notify target or a Home Assistant notification, who came and went and when, guest tags that arrived or left, the rooms empty longest, and lights left on in rooms where nobody was; you tick which of these it includes. The figures are Traceback's for that day, and the message links to Traceback on that day. A quiet day gets "Nothing to report", never an empty message, and a restart never sends a day twice. Free in every edition.

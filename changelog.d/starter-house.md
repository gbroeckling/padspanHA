# Start with a rough house from your Home Assistant rooms

### New
- **A first map with no drawing at all.** While no room is drawn yet, Setup (at the top) and Mapping's empty Library offer **Start with a rough house from your Home Assistant rooms**. **Show what it will make** lists each floor with its rooms, and what it leaves out and why; **Make the rough house** then puts one square box for each Home Assistant area on its floor, named as the area, on a blank floor marked "rough start" for each floor that has no plan yet. A box is 3.5 m across, a little bigger for an area with many devices. Your devices show in their rooms straight away. An area with no floor in Home Assistant goes on the floor you pick. It only runs when you press it, it is free, and it never touches calibration, receivers or walls, or a room or plan you already have; a second press makes nothing.
- **The boxes are a starting point.** The rough plan's note says how many rooms are still boxes and offers **Reshape the rooms** (Mapping → Layout in Advanced; in Basic the Setup Wizard's rooms step, with the boxes drawn dashed) and **Upload a plan of this floor**. A room you trace, on the rough plan or on a real plan uploaded later, and put on the map with **Put it on the map** takes the place of its box under the same name, so it keeps its Home Assistant area. A box you have reshaped is yours and is never replaced.
- **In the opt-in report:** whether the rough house was offered, previewed and made (with how many rooms, as a band), and which way on was taken afterwards. Words and counts only.

# The PadSpan HA Manual: start, the map, calibration, every day

### New
- **What PadSpan is**, a new chapter of the manual in `docs/manual/` on GitHub: free and Pro, the editions, how it compares, and an "is it for me?" page that says plainly what Bluetooth can and can't do.
- **Requirements**: Home Assistant 2024.8 or newer, memory and Low-memory mode, and every kind of receiver, ESPresense and Tasmota included, with the one switch or command each needs.
- **Your first room in 15 minutes**: from install to Who's where now, a receiver's room picked right on the card, naming your phone, and on to a map.
- **Setup, step by step**: the plan, its scale, rooms, floor heights, receivers (with a room for one that has none), your phone or watch, Put it on the map and calibration; five ways to a plan when you have none; lining up floors.
- **Calibration**: when to calibrate and how many spots, Walk a room and how to turn it on, Guided calibration, Beacon Tune, reading Results and Accuracy by spot, and "Signals say this receiver is in …".
- **The screens**: Overview, Live Tracker, Follow, Occupancy, Traceback, Forensics, Health and the rest; Basic, Advanced and More tools; the sample house. Its menu map is made from the panel itself, and a test fails when the two disagree.
- **Alerts and notifications, all in one place**: Follow's movement alerts, the leak and smoke alarm banner, Alert pulse, Vacation Mode and guest notices, and Home Assistant's Repairs.

# Live Aboard opens on the whole house, labels by zoom level, Glass walls

### New
- **Glass walls.** Walls has a fourth choice, **Glass**: the walls between you and the rooms turn into see-through tinted glass instead of being cut down, so each room keeps its height, and the walls at the back stay solid. Double-tap a room and the inside walls of its floor turn to glass too. The screen you pick it on keeps it, and it's also a line in ⋯ Show on this screen (**Glass walls**).
- **Labels by zoom level.** Looking at the whole house, each floor gets one tag beside it with its name and what's going on there ("Main · 3 lights on · 1 open · 2 people"); tap a tag to see that floor alone. On one floor you see room names, readings and sensor markers. Double-tap a room (or zoom in on it) and you see the device codes in that room while the other rooms fade back. Before, every name, reading, code and receiver showed at once. Leak, smoke, CO and gas alarms always show.

### Changed
- **Live Aboard opens on the whole house.** In the sidebar it now opens on every floor, pulled apart, with the page's bars out of the way so the house fills the window from the start. Tap ☰ (top right) to bring the bars back. If you had turned ⇕ Explode off, it stays off and the view opens on the floor with the most rooms, as before. A home view you set is still where it opens. Wall screens and Mapping open as they did.
- **A floor you pick stays picked.** Choosing a floor right after Live Aboard opens is no longer undone when the map finishes loading.

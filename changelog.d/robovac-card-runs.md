# The robot vacuum's card, and room location runs

### New
- **The robot vacuum has its own card.** With a Valetudo robot linked, press and hold the robot on the Atlas or in the 3D house to open it. It talks to the robot's own Valetudo (no vacuum entity or MQTT needed): what the robot is doing, its battery, and Start, Pause and Dock. If Valetudo's login is on, the card asks for its user and password and says so plainly when the robot answers 401. For administrators; a tap on the robot still shows its small card.
- **You choose how the robot's points are used.** Before the first run the card asks: count them like your own, fill gaps and count them less (only in rooms where you have fewer than 3 points of your own, at half weight), or use them for Find mistakes and Wi-Fi only. Nothing is picked for you. Change it any time; the card says what that would change for earlier runs and lets you apply it in one undoable step, or leave them. After each run it shows how your own test spots scored before and after.
- **Room location runs (optional, off until you start one).** Stick a fixed-address Bluetooth beacon (iBeacon or Eddystone, not an AirTag or a Tile) on top of the robot, and the card's Room location part sends the robot to spots about 1.5 m apart in every room (or just one), clear of the walls. At each spot it stands still for about 20 seconds while PadSpan listens to the beacon, and the spot is added to calibration. Calibration is backed up before every run, Stop sends the robot back to its dock, and Undo takes out exactly the points one run added, never your own; every run whose points are still there keeps its own Undo. If the robot heads home on its own or its battery drops below 20 %, the run ends there (and below 20 % none starts). The card shows what is missing first: the robot's address, its map from Valetudo, and whether the beacon is heard right now. On a wall screen it shows the robot's state only.

- **Find mistakes.** A button on the robot's card opens a card of its own that lists receivers placed in the wrong spot or on the wrong floor, worked out from the points the runs already collected (no extra driving): which receiver, where it is placed, where the readings put it, how sure, and a link to its placement. A receiver is judged only from spots lying round it; the others are listed as not enough readings. Nothing is moved.
- **The robot's Wi-Fi at each spot.** During a run the robot also reports its Wi-Fi signal at each spot; PadSpan keeps the last 2,000 for the Wi-Fi coverage map, and other integrations can read them. A spot where the Wi-Fi can't be read is not skipped.

- **Runs listen harder and stop cleanly.** At each stop PadSpan counts every packet each receiver hears from the beacon (Home Assistant 2026.10 and newer; older versions read each receiver once a second), drops a stop the robot moved during, skips a spot the robot can't reach, takes a stop where the robot settles close to its target, keeps clear of your no-go areas, virtual walls and obstacles, and ends a run sending nothing more when someone or a Valetudo timer takes the robot over. A run won't start if the robot's map has changed since it was imported, and the card says when a Valetudo timer is due within two hours. Find mistakes now calls a receiver "on the wrong floor" when it hears the robot about as strongly as the receivers on the robot's own floor do.

### Changed
- **The robot is always on the map while its link is on.** With Robot Vacuum Import on, the robot is drawn on the Atlas and in the 3D house whether people and tags are shown or not, so its card is always one hold away.
- **The robot's points are PadSpan's, not yours.** In every count that tells your own calibration points from automatic ones, the robot's points count as automatic. The beacons' own clean-ups never remove them; only a run's Undo does.

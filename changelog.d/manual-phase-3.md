# The PadSpan HA Manual is complete: receivers, phones, tags, lights, the 3D house, running it

### New
- **The manual in `docs/manual/` on GitHub has every chapter now**, from what PadSpan is to getting help, with the contents page linking each one.
- **Receivers, phones and tags**: which receivers to use and how many (ESPHome, Shelly, Home Assistant's own Bluetooth, ESPresense, Tasmota), where to put them, and PadSpan next to Bermuda; Android phones, iPhones and Apple Watches step by step; which tags work, AirTags, and lending a guest tag.
- **Rooms and floors come from Home Assistant**: how areas and floors become PadSpan's rooms, how floors are stacked, and the Rooms screen. **Walls and signal loss**: the materials, open edges, and steel doors that open with their sensor.
- **The Atlas** (with power teams: a wall dimmer and its smart bulbs as one light, Follow the day and a wake-up), **Live Aboard**, **Furnish** and **Wall screens and kiosks**, each in one chapter.
- **AI features**: connecting an AI Task or Claude, exactly what is read and where it goes, and what it costs.
- **Users and Enhanced security**, **Performance and small machines**, **Privacy: what leaves the house**, and **PadSpan Pro** (trial, keys, homes, testers, invites).
- **Appendices**: every screen and its tabs, the accuracy benchmark, and a part for developers with every websocket command. The screens table and the command table are made from the code, and a test fails when either no longer matches it.

# The panel names Home Assistant's Areas, labels & zones menu

### Fixed
- **The panel sends you to the menu Home Assistant actually has.** Nine places in the panel (Follow, Mapping, the Overview's receiver card, QA, Settings and Training) called Home Assistant's menu for rooms and floors "Areas & Zones"; on every Home Assistant PadSpan supports it is **Areas, labels & zones**.
- **Training's Quick Start names the Marketplace.** It only said to install through HACS and restart; on Home Assistant 2026.11 and newer you add PadSpan from Settings → Marketplace and its setup opens right away, with no restart. HACS, and the restart after it, is for older versions.

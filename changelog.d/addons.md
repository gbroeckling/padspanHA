# Add-ons

### New
- **Add-ons, for installs without Pro.** One feature each, one payment that covers 2 years, no subscription, up to three homes. PadSpan Pro includes them all. They share one key, entered in Settings → Features → PadSpan licence like any other; the card lists each add-on with its date.
  - **Audio** ($6.45 CAD): volume, mute and off for each room's players in the room's card, and a speaker beside a playing room's name.
  - **Alarm & security** ($6.45): Arm and Disarm in the emergency button, the emergency lights test (the button, its list and Force off), the door, garage and gate card on the map, marking openers in Devices → Door Openers, building an opener or a lock from relays, and linking an opener or a lock to a door in the Atlas builder.
  - **Wi-Fi coverage** ($6.45): the Wi-Fi coverage tab.
  - **Climate map** ($8.65): the ♨ Heatmap on the Atlas.
  - **Time travel** ($7.55): Full house activity, the day replayed on the Atlas.
  - **Vacation Mode & presets** ($4.25): Vacation Mode and the Whole house presets.
  - **Looks pack** ($4.25): the Graphite, Ocean, Dusk and Day themes, and on the Atlas Showcase with its themes, the West Coast Forest and the Look presets.
  - **Forensics** ($5.56): Forensics recording.
  - **Presence insights** ($5.56): Busy Times and Locate.
- **Coming with the free 3D house: Advanced map creation** ($8.65 CAD), not on sale yet: AI plan reading (the Plans tool, Line up plans, Rooms from plans and Let Claude draw your rooms), reading a photo, a LiDAR scan's doors, windows, furniture and stairs in the 3D house, Furniture from scans, and floor plans from a LiDAR scan. PadSpan Pro includes it. Until it is on sale nothing changes for anyone: the first of these stay PadSpan Pro's, and a floor plan from a LiDAR scan stays free.
- **Audio on the classic layout.** Settings → Atlas → Audio, off by default, does for the whole house what the ⋯ list's Audio line does per screen on the new layout: a speaker beside a playing room's name, and Audio in the room's card with Match rooms for an administrator. It shows where Audio is allowed.

### Changed
- **The emergency lights test moved into Alarm & security.** It was free; it now needs PadSpan Pro or the Alarm & security add-on, and Home Assistant refuses its test, Force off and switches without either. A test already running always shows, and can be ended or forced off. Still free on every install: an alarm going off, shown in the button with nothing to press, the red emergency banner for a leak, smoke, CO, gas or an alarm, locking and unlocking on the map and in the room card, and Alert pulse.
- **Each paid feature asks for its own add-on.** Where a feature was PadSpan Pro's alone, it now says "PadSpan Pro or the … add-on", with the add-on's price. A Bright Pro key keeps everything it had.
- **Full house activity draws what the install may show.** Replayed on the Atlas, placed lights and Showcase need what they need on the Atlas itself, so Time travel alone replays the free map.
- **The licence card names an add-on key as such.** It never calls it Pro, and offers the add-ons in one list beside Pro to an install without Pro. An install with an add-on key can still start the Pro trial: its key is kept aside and comes back by itself when the trial ends, after its 14 days' grace; a trial bought in place keeps its paid key. Every licence check now says which add-ons this PadSpan knows, so the licence server answers an add-on key only to a PadSpan that can tell it from Pro.

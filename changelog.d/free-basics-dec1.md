# The 3D house free from December 1; Robot vacuum and Advanced map creation add-ons

### New
- **From December 1 (00:00 Pacific) the basics are free, no key needed:** placing each light and device exactly where it is on the map, the plain wall screen, and the 3D house (Live Aboard). On an install without a key, Live Aboard comes on once by itself; each screen still opens on the flat map until 3D is picked there. PadSpan Pro keeps fixture shapes and Showcase, the 3D editor, Furnish, people in 3D and the director, and the wall's night dimming, burn-in shift, floors in turn and Fully Kiosk motion wake. It switches by itself at that moment, without a restart.
- **A one-time Overview card, "The 3D house is now free",** for an administrator without Pro, from December 1. It shows once and never on a wall screen.
- **Two add-ons go on sale on December 1:** Advanced map creation, and the new Robot vacuum add-on. Robot vacuum covers your robot vacuum's map as a floor plan, the robot on the Atlas map and in 3D with its card, room location runs, the mistake finder and Wi-Fi while it cleans. Both are half price until January 1, like the other add-ons.
- **30% off the other one:** if you hold Advanced map creation you pay 30% less for Robot vacuum, and the other way round. You get the better of this and the sale price, never both. The licence card (Settings → Features → PadSpan licence) links straight to the payment at that price. Pay with the email your key was sent to.

### Changed
- **The robot-vacuum features are free until December 1.** From then on they need PadSpan Pro, the Robot vacuum add-on or a Bright Pro key. Without one, the robot is not drawn, and the import and the runs say what they need. Nothing you stored is deleted: you can still stop or undo a run and turn the link off, and it all comes back when a key is entered.

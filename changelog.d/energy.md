# Energy, a new add-on (not sold yet)

### New
- **Energy: each room's power on the Atlas, from Home Assistant's own Energy setup.** Turn it on in Settings → Atlas → Energy (off by default: until then nothing of it shows anywhere and nothing is read). A metered room gets a small "⚡ 120 W" beside its name, a device placed on the map shows its watts, and a room's card lists each metered device with its watts now, its kWh and cost today, and the room's total. A device plugged into another metered one is taken out of it, so nothing is counted twice. On the new layout and on a wall screen each screen ticks Energy in its ⋯ list; on the classic layout one switch does it for the whole house. There is nothing to import: PadSpan reads Home Assistant's Energy settings as they are, and if Energy isn't set up there it says so and links to it. It works without Bluetooth receivers.
- **Watts in empty rooms.** The new Energy view shows the house's watts now and the rest of the house beside the metered devices, today's rooms and devices with their cost, and the power used in rooms nobody has been in for 15 minutes (you choose how long): "Empty for 2 h: 85 W (about $0.40 today)". It goes by PadSpan's own presence, by the rooms' motion and occupancy sensors when there are no receivers, and by everyone's Home Assistant person being away. Devices you expect to run all the time (a fridge, network gear) can be left out.
- **Standby while you're out.** While everyone is away or Vacation Mode is on, PadSpan measures the house's lowest steady use and says what it costs over a year at your price.
- **Sensor health.** Stale readings, units that aren't W, kW, kWh or Wh, a power and an energy sensor that disagree, a device bigger than the one it is part of, a grid figure that exports at night and a device with no area are each listed with the sensor.
- **Who was home at spikes** (administrators only, off by default): the biggest jumps in the house's power over the last 7 days, with who PadSpan saw at home and where, and a link to Traceback at that moment. It stays on your house.
- **Yesterday at home** adds the kWh and cost used in empty rooms the day before, while Energy is on.
- Energy is an add-on (Energie in German). It isn't on sale yet; PadSpan Pro includes it.

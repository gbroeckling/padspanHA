# Setup's tools and Calibration in German, French and Dutch

### New
- **Setup's tools and Calibration speak German, French and Dutch.** When your Home Assistant profile is set to one of them, Mapping (Upload, Edit with all its tools, 3D Stack with Floor Heights and Point Align, Layout), setting a plan's scale, the ways to a plan when you have none, the Setup Wizard's Put it on the map, the offers of Let Claude draw your rooms, and all of Calibration (guided calibration, Pin & Listen, Roam, Model, Results, Accuracy by spot, Walk a room, Tune and Beacon Tune) are in your language.
- **The rest of the device screens too.** Devices' By Room, Registry, Door Openers and relay wizard, the "same device?" lines and address badges, the Bluetooth page's Scanners, Advertisements, Visualisation and ESPHome configs tabs, and the overhead 2D map.
- Settings, Manage, Training, the Atlas and the 3D view are still in English; they come next.

### Fixed
- **The Overview's map is read out again.** The hidden text that tells a screen reader who is in which room stopped filling in after the last round of translations.
- **Mapping's receivers step says how to add a receiver.** It said to double-click the map, which made a marker Put it on the map does not use; it now says to press Add beside the receiver in Live BLE Receivers, then drag it into place.

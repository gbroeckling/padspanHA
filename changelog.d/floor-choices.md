# Only this floor on the flat Atlas: the choices settled

### Changed
- **Leak and open-window rings stay bright on a faded floor,** so an alarm is never dimmed by choosing another floor.
- **A door or window circle you are placing in Mapping now fades with its floor** and takes no taps. Before, it stayed bright and draggable on a faded floor. Choose its floor to drag it; a message says so if you tap where its nearest wall is on another floor. Save opening and Discard work either way.
- **Choosing a floor no longer scrolls the sidebar Atlas to it,** from a chip or a saved view.
- **A queued light that belongs on another floor isn't placed.** With a floor chosen in Mapping, tapping that floor's plate with a light from another floor places nothing, says "W18 belongs on Upper. Choose that floor first." and keeps it in the queue. With All floors chosen, placing is as before.

### Fixed
- **A saved Atlas floor now applies when the page opens.** The Atlas opened on All and only showed the floor kept by Save view after a Refresh, because it checked the floor before it had read the house. If that floor is no longer there, the Atlas opens on All. Mapping opened before the settings arrive now takes the saved view when they do.

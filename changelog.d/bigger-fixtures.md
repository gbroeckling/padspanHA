# 3D look: bigger fixtures, and buttons at the placed spot

### Changed
- **Round light fixtures are drawn bigger in the 3D look.** A pot light was drawn its real 15 cm and a plain light as a 7 cm ball: a few pixels from across a room. A pot, a dome and a single-bulb light are now never drawn smaller than 26 cm across (at most three times their size). Strips, tubes, panels and chandeliers keep their size.
- **A light that is off is solid and lighter.** It was half see-through and leaned to the theme's dark colour, so it all but vanished at night.
- **⋯ → "Lights as buttons": a button now sits at the spot the light was placed on the map**, with its line to the nearest part of the fixture as drawn. It sat under the middle of what was drawn, which for a ring of pots round a room is nowhere near any of them, and for a strip drawn on a wall can be metres from its spot.

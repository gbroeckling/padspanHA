# When a plan is moved, what was placed on it can move too

### Added
- **Mapping → Library says when a moved plan has left things behind.** A floor plan's place in the house is corrected now and then: its scale was wrong, its shape squeezed, it was lined up again. Everything put on it (the room traced on it, the receivers pinned on it, the calibration points recorded on it, the lights and sensors in its rooms) is stored in metres, so it stayed where the plan used to be. The plan's row now says so: "This plan was moved: 1 room, 3 receivers and 32 calibration points placed on it still stand where it used to be, up to 1.7 m off."
- **Review lists what could move with it, with nothing ticked.** Tick what should follow the plan and each goes to the same spot on the picture it was on. A backup is taken first, and a move is refused if the backup can't be made. A calibration point's readings, room and label are never touched: only its place.
- **"Leave them where they are"** ends it for that plan without moving anything.

### Usage report (opt-in)
- The line shown, the list opened, which kinds were moved, left as they are, or a move refused. Words only.

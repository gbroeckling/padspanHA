# Limits on names, notes, positions and lists

### Fixed
- **PadSpan no longer keeps text, numbers or lists no home would have.** Sent by a script or an automation calling PadSpan directly (the panel never sends them), PadSpan stored as it came: a name or label of 100,000 characters or with an invisible NUL character in it, a receiver or beacon at a position of 1e308 metres, a list of 10,000 followed devices or hidden plans, and settings nested 200 levels deep. Every save, backup and screen then carried them. These are now refused in words, before anything changes. The limits are far past anything a home uses: a name, label, room or id up to 200 characters, a note up to 2,000, a position within 10 km of the house's origin, a room's height within the same, up to 500 rooms and 1,000 pins on a plan in one save, up to 5,000 entries in a setting's list, and up to 10 levels in a record sent whole.
- **A wall keeps only what a wall is.** A wall saved from the panel or a script kept every field it was sent, whatever its name. Now it keeps its name, points, floor, material, how much it weakens a signal, its railing, its door or window sensor, opener and lock, and whether that sensor reads backwards. A wall's name is up to 200 characters and a wall up to 1,000 points.

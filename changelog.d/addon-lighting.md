# Advanced smart lighting, the eleventh add-on

### New
- **Advanced smart lighting, an add-on: $8.65 CAD, one payment for 2 years ($4.32 in the Black Friday sale, until January 1).** It holds the light design tools and the smart side of power teams: on the Atlas, WLED's Advanced controls, Automorph, Fit room, Light spread, Scene, Ripple, Hide untouched, fixture shapes and the type override (with Showcase in its Classic theme as their stage); for power teams, making, changing and removing a team, following the day, Sleep, the bedtime, the night light and the wake-up. Placing a light where it really is stays PadSpan Pro's (and Bright Pro's). PadSpan Pro includes it, and a Bright Pro key keeps everything it had.

### Changed
- **Power teams without Pro or the add-on keep working as one light.** A team already made still switches on and off, dims, cuts and restores its power, and shows each bulb with Try again. Without the add-on it no longer follows the day, sleeps or wakes, and nobody can make, change or remove a team; Settings shows the teams as they are and offers the add-on. A wake-up or bedtime fading when a licence ends finishes the fade already sent to the bulbs, then the lights stay as they are, and the day leads again by itself once a key is back.

### Fixed
- **A key of add-ons newer than your PadSpan reads as an add-on key, never as Pro.** PadSpan now keeps the licence server's word that a key is an add-on key, so one holding only add-ons this version doesn't know yet is never shown as "PadSpan Pro licensed", and the 90-day Pro trial is still offered. When the server says the key needs a newer PadSpan, the licence card says "This add-on needs a newer PadSpan — update PadSpan." and nothing is stored as Pro.

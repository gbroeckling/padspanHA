# Rooms as light switches

### New
- **Rooms as light switches, in the 3D look (⋯ → Lights, off unless you tick it).** At night the 3D look is as dark as the house, so a light that is off is a dark dot nobody can find or hit. With this ticked, a room's floor is the switch for its lights: tap it and they go on together, or all off if any is on (the message has Undo). Hold for the room's list, where each light has its own switch. Hold, then drag up or down, to dim the room. And a touch lights the house up as by day for 20 seconds, so everything can be seen; on a dark house the first touch only does that. Sensors, locks and doors keep their own taps. On the Atlas tab and its wall screens, per screen.

# German leftovers on Rooms and Traceback

### Fixed
- **Rooms in German: "Art des Raums" no longer covers "Empfänger ansehen".** The kind-of-space list was as wide as its longest choice, and in German that choice ran over the Receivers field beside it. The list now stays inside its own column (the outdoor surface list too), on a wide screen and a phone.
- **Traceback speaks German everywhere.** "No traceback frames loaded", the empty-range and load-failure messages, the 5/15/30 min buttons, "to" between the dates, the minutes box, the Insights and Busy Times buttons and the Vacation Mode tooltip were still in English with German loaded.

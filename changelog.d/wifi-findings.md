# Wi-Fi coverage says what to fix

### New
- **Wi-Fi coverage (test): what to fix, first.** Above the map, UniFi Utopia's findings, most urgent first, each with what to do: placed devices that keep dropping off Wi-Fi, that sit on a far access point while a neighbour a few metres away hears another one much better, that are very weak, PadSpan receivers on weak Wi-Fi (their reports arrive late or not at all), rooms where a phone spends time on weak Wi-Fi, and before and after for a device that moved to another access point. A click shows the finding on the plan.
- **Access points on the plan.** Each access point is drawn where you put it, or (dashed) where the devices that hear it best suggest. An administrator drags one to where it really is, or sets its floor under "Where the access points are".

### Changed
- **UniFi Blocker is now UniFi Utopia.** The Wi-Fi coverage tab asks UniFi Utopia (`unifi_utopia/...`) and names it.

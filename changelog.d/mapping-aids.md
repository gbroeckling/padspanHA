# Drawing rooms: corners that line up, straight edges, overlaps named

### New
- **Corners land on other rooms.** In Mapping → Edit, a room corner you click near another room's corner lands exactly on it; near another room's edge, it lands on the edge; otherwise, on a plan with a scale, it lands on a 5 cm grid. In Mapping → Layout a corner you drag does the same. Hold Alt to put a corner exactly where you click or drop it. On a scanned plan, a corner near a scanned wall end still lands on the wall end first.
- **Straighten edges.** A new button in Edit (Rooms tool) and in Layout. Every edge that is less than 5 cm off level or upright is made straight, and a corner another room shares moves with it, so a corner clicked 2 cm off no longer makes a slanted wall in 3D. Edges shorter than half a metre the other way are left alone, since a slant there may be meant. In Layout, "straight" means square to that floor's own walls, so a floor placed turned a degree or two is straightened to itself, not bent. It says how many edges it straightened, and nothing is saved until you press Save Layout (Edit) or Save corrections (Layout); Revert or Discard changes takes it back. In Edit it needs the plan's scale.
- **Rooms that overlap are named.** Edit and Layout now say, for example, "Kitchen and Pantry overlap, so the walls between them can't be drawn." It checks what is on screen, unsaved changes included. The usage report already counted these by number; now you see which rooms. In Edit this needs the plan's scale.
- **Help.** Mapping's Edit and Layout help now say how corners line up, how to place one freely with Alt, and what Straighten edges does (English, Deutsch, Français, Nederlands).

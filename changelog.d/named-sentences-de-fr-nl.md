# Sentences with a name in them, in German, French and Dutch

### Changed
- **Health's problems that name a room, a plan or a receiver are in your language.** "Kitchen and Hall get mixed up", "Receiver “Hall proxy” often points to the wrong room", "Positions on “Ground” are often off" and the other System Critics that put a name or a number into their sentence used to stay in English in German, French and Dutch. The names stay as you gave them; numbers are written your language's way.
- **So are the most common refusals with a name in them**, such as "Kitchen is not a room on the map.", "There's no area called … in Home Assistant.", a file over the upload limit, a notification target that is gone, an IRK already saved for a phone, the usage report on a free licence, and the WLED team refusals.

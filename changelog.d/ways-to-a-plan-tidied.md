# The ways to a floor plan, tidied

### Changed
- **"Ways to get a floor plan" opens straight under its own button**, above the upload form, in place of below the whole page. A room scan and a blank floor each ask which floor they are for, so nothing points at another part of the screen.
- **Handing the scan to someone else's phone is one box.** The guide's address sits in a field with a Copy button, beside "Guide me step by step".
- **Setup's first step and the empty Library say there are other ways** when you have no floor plan: a phone's room scan, your own sketch or a blank floor.
- **Mapping → Layout's floor plan import takes a RoomPlan USDZ as well as its JSON**, while RoomPlan Import is on in Settings: the one reader takes both.

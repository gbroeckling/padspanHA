# Settings' Appearance, Scanner Map and Tracking in German, French and Dutch

### New
- **Half of Settings speaks German, French and Dutch.** When your Home Assistant profile is set to one of them, the Settings tab strip and its tips, Appearance, Scanner Map and all of Tracking are in your language: room changes and timeouts, phones and guest tags (with the Phone Tracking setup guide), what the panel shows, positioning and calibration, receivers, performance, integrations (Automations, HA Tags, the Assist questions card, Ignore Bermuda) and privacy (Update Check, Enhanced security, the usage report).
- **Paths in the help and messages name the tabs as you see them.** "Settings → Tracking → Receivers" now reads in your language wherever the panel or its help says it.
- The Assist questions stay in English: Assist knows them only from the English sentences file, and the card says so.
- Settings' Features, AI, Atlas, UI Structure and licence tabs, Manage, Training, the Atlas and the 3D view are still in English; they come next.

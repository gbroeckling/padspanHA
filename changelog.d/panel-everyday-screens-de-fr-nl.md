# The everyday screens in German, French and Dutch

### New
- **More of the panel speaks German, French and Dutch.** When your Home Assistant profile is set to one of them, the menu and the phone's top bar, the messages that pop up, the update banner and What's new, the Overview, Who's where now, Follow (with Locate), Occupancy, Health with its list of problems, the status dot, the Receivers page, the IRK Manager, the Devices list and the device and receiver sheets now show their words in your language. The release notes themselves stay in English.
- **Problems and refusals in your language.** When PadSpan says no to something, or Health lists a problem, the sentence is shown in your language too. A sentence that names one of your rooms or devices is still in English for now.

# A Zigbee device in ZHA and Zigbee2MQTT counts once

### New
- **The same Zigbee device in Home Assistant twice is used once.** A device paired through Zigbee2MQTT and still in ZHA (or deCONZ) has the same Zigbee address in both; PadSpan now treats the two as one unit and uses the Zigbee2MQTT one. The other copy is left out of the Atlas, the light index, the room counts and every list where you pick a device (Settings → Atlas's rain sensor and weather lists, door and lock links, door openers, power teams, Evening lights, Live Aboard's Follows and "This is a device…"), with a quiet "1 duplicate hidden (same unit on Zigbee2MQTT)" where a list is shown. The coordinator sticks are never counted as duplicates.
- **A copy you already picked or placed says so.** It stays where it is, with a line naming the Zigbee2MQTT one and a button, Use the Zigbee2MQTT one; nothing changes until you press it. In Mapping → Atlas builder the button moves the device's place on the map, or its wall link, to the Zigbee2MQTT one.
- **Told when it happens.** When a new device turns out to be the same unit as one already in another integration, Home Assistant shows a notification naming both copies, once for each pair. PadSpan notices it from Home Assistant's own device list as the device is added (no polling) and never changes Home Assistant itself.

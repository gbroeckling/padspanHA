# The usage report: power teams and colour bulbs

### New
- **The opt-in usage report says how power teams are used, and which colour bulbs houses have.** A new line in the report, `power_teams`: how many power teams a house has, whether each has a wall control and what kind (a light or a switch, and which integration runs it, such as Control4, Lutron or Z-Wave), whether turning it off cuts the power or turns the bulbs off, whether it follows the day, has its own curve or a wake-up, and its bulbs by brand and integration. Team or not, it also counts the house's colour bulbs by brand (Hue, IKEA, Innr, LIDL, Ledvance, WiZ, Tuya and others) and by integration (a Hue Bridge, Zigbee2MQTT, ZHA and others), so the developer can see which bulbs people have. The power team card on the map also counts its buttons (opened, power cut or put back, Day, Sleep, Try again, a setting saved) and taps on the map that switch a team. Counts and words from a fixed list only, never a team's name, an entity, a time of day or a maker's own words. It is sent only with the report on, the Preview shows it, and the README's list of what the report sends has the new lines.

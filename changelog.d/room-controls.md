# Room controls on the Atlas

### New
- **Room controls: a room's music, shades and thermostat in its sheet.** Tick **Room controls** in the Atlas map's ⋯ (per screen, off unless ticked). A room's sheet then shows its temperature, humidity and who is there, a **Room off** button for its lights and players, an **Audio** part with each player's mute, volume − and +, level and off, one **Shades** row (open, stop, close all of them) and each thermostat's setpoint with − and +. Every press is one call to Home Assistant; anyone who can switch the lights can use them, wall screens too. Off, the map and the sheets are exactly as before.
- **A speaker beside a room's name while a player there is on.** With room controls on, a small speaker shows beside the name of a room whose player is on, struck through while the room's first player is muted, and nothing while they are all off. A tap on it opens the room's sheet.
- **Match players to rooms, Control4 and the other whole-house systems.** Control4, Crestron, Savant, Russound, Nuvo, Monoprice, Xantech, Triad, Denon, Yamaha and Anthem players are recognised by their Home Assistant integration. An administrator matches them to the map's rooms once (**Match rooms**, or **Match Control4 to HA** in a Control4 house), with first guesses from each player's area or name. Two players can share a room, a Control4 zone and a Denon zone say, in the order you set. **Include Home Assistant speakers** (off unless ticked) adds Music Assistant, ESPHome, Cast and TV players.

# Railings only where you choose them

### Changed
- **A new staircase has no rail until you pick one.** Choose Both, Left/Inside or Right/Outside on its panel in Furnish. Stairs you already have keep the rail they show (Auto, offered only on those until you change it); stairs a stair room or the AI suggests start with none.
- **An Open (Loft) edge you draw from now on starts without a railing.** The Railing button on its row in Signal walls gives it one, or takes it away, outright. Edges you already have keep the railing they show (over a hall that rises past them) until you set it.

### New
- **Two more rail styles for stairs.** Beside the balustrade (a handrail on spindles): Topless, the spindles and posts with no top rail, and Single bar, one bar with nothing under it.

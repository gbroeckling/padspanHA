# Calmer bare floors in 3D; a tidier emergency button

### Changed
- **Live Aboard: bare rooms no longer look carpeted.** A room with nothing in it showed its colour and its motion glow as one flat, strong fill, so most rooms of a house not yet furnished read as navy, green or magenta carpets. A room's floor now takes its colour more softly the less furniture stands in it, and a bare room's motion glow is a lighter fill with the same bright edge. Each room keeps its own colour, lit lights and motion stand out more, and furnished rooms change only a little. In Classic, Studio and every Showcase look.
- **The Test emergency lighting button's corner.** The small list mark (tap the ring for every emergency light) now sits inside the ring instead of over its corner, and ☰ under the button has a clear gap and lines up with it, in Live Aboard and the flat Atlas.

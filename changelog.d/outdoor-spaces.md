# Covered patios, open decks and the Outside floor

### New
- **Say what kind of space a room is.** On the Rooms page, each room on a floor of the house has a Kind of space choice: Indoors, Open deck or patio (open to the sky), or Covered patio or porch (a roof, deck or floor over it, open sides). Left on "Work it out", PadSpan reads the name as before (deck, patio, porch...) and shows what it worked out. In the 3D view a covered patio stays dry in the rain and snow, and its ceiling lights hang from what covers it instead of being set into the floor; a porch you mark Covered with nothing above it gets a thin roof of its own.
- **A deck or patio belongs to the floor it is level with.** The Outside floor is for what is on no level of the house, such as a shed, a driveway or a garden. A deck or patio put on the Outside floor gets a short reminder on the Rooms page; nothing is moved.

### Changed
- **A patio under a deck or a room above is now taken as covered.** In the 3D view, rain and snow no longer fall on it, and pots, pendants and fans in it hang from the floor above. To have it drawn open again, choose "Open deck or patio" for that room on the Rooms page. Positioning, presence and calibration are unchanged: a covered patio is still outdoors for them.

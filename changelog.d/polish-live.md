# Live Tracker, Bluetooth and Traceback tidied up, on a phone and on a wide screen

### Fixed
- **Live Tracker: the search box no longer sits on the scanner strip.** The counter (rooms, tracked, radios), the search box and the scanner strip over the map could land on top of each other: on a 1280-wide screen the search hid the first scanner codes, and on a phone it hid the counter as well. They now share one row that never overlaps. With room, each is where it was. With less, the search moves over; on a phone the counter and the search share the first line and the scanner strip takes the second.
- **Bluetooth, "Who hears what": text you can read at any width.** The graph's text grew and shrank with the window: about 4 px on a phone, about 19 px on a wide monitor. It is now the same size as the rest of the page everywhere. Names are no longer cut at 38 letters with free space beside them: a name is shortened only when the graph is too narrow to show it, as on a phone.
- **Bluetooth, "Who hears what": device names no longer drawn over each other.** Where two scanners sat close together, their devices could land on the same line.
- **Bluetooth on a phone: the tabs line up with the cards.** The tab strip was a few pixels wider than the cards above it and its two rows were uneven. It now ends where the cards end, three equal tabs over two equal ones. The Search and Source labels sit on one line.
- **Traceback: the legend under the map names the floors.** It listed the file name of every floor plan, joined with "+", and ran off the right edge ("4 Posi"). It now shows each floor's name (Lower, Main, Upper) and stays inside the map. A level whose plans belong to no named floor still shows the plans' names, shortened to fit.
- **Traceback: a tag no longer covers the name of the room it is in.** A tag's marker and its room chip ("Garage") sat right on the room's own name ("Garage"). The marker now stands just above the name (or just below it), on Playback and on New Objects.
- **Traceback on a phone: tidy tabs, and "Filter:" in one piece.** Playback, New Objects, Insights and Busy Times each stay on one line and share a row, with Full house activity on the next, instead of labels wrapped over two and three lines at different heights. Under Distance Traveled, "Filter:" no longer breaks into "Filte" and "r:".

# Only this floor on the flat Atlas, and which page themes reach

### Changed
- **Choosing a floor on the flat Atlas fades the others.** Before, only the other floors' plates dimmed: their rooms, names and lights stayed at full strength, yet most of their lights ignored a tap. Now those rooms, names and lights fade to a faint backdrop and ignore taps, so what looks tappable is tappable. Each floor's number badge stays bright and still opens that floor's list. A leak or open-window ring and the ring that flashes a light's spot stay bright too, on any floor. All looks exactly as it did. The fade also shows on Mapping → Atlas and Traceback's House activity, which draw the same map. A door or window circle you are placing in Mapping fades with its floor too, so choose that floor to drag it. While a floor is chosen in Mapping, a tap with a light from the placement queue or a drop of the pin counts only on that floor's plate; anywhere else it does nothing, so choose the other floor or All to place there. A queued light that belongs on another floor isn't placed: a message says to choose its floor first, and it stays in the queue.

### Fixed
- **A saved Atlas floor now applies when the page opens.** The Atlas opened on All and only showed the floor kept by Save view after a Refresh, because it checked the floor before it had read the house. If that floor is no longer there, the Atlas opens on All.
- **Settings and the help now say the sidebar Atlas page stays dark.** Settings said a theme applied in every browser and offered Light to anyone who can't use a dark screen, but the sidebar Atlas page never takes a theme. It stays dark on purpose, Light (inverted) included, and Settings → Theme and the help now say so.

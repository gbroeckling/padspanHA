# Small rooms beside bigger ones

### Fixed
- **A small room along the wall of a bigger one shows sooner.** With your house drawn, a device counts for the next room only once its position is 0.75 m past the wall. A small room such as a bathroom along a bedroom wall is often only 2 m deep, and the position wanders a metre or two, so a stay there was sometimes never shown. Such a room now needs a quarter of its depth (0.5 m for 2 m). Rooms 3 m deep or more, and hallways, keep 0.75 m, so a device standing just inside a doorway does not flicker.

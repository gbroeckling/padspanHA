# Help pages brought up to date (Calibration, Rooms and Setup)

### Changed
- **Calibration's help matches the screen again.** It starts from Results and Guided calibration, names every tab as it is now, gives one answer for how long to stand (15 s to start, 60 s recommended) and how many spots (10–20 per floor), and says plainly which buttons delete calibration points. The old standalone calibration panel, which no longer exists, is gone from it.
- **New ? help** on Calibration's Results tab, Guided calibration, Beacon Tune, Accuracy by spot and Walk a room, and on the Rooms and Setup screens. Results explains why a room can read higher than the house's headline figure.
- **Traceback, Insights and Busy Times help rewritten**: what is recorded (named and followed devices, 7 days), the Run time slider, New Objects, and that Insights and Busy Times work without a floor plan.

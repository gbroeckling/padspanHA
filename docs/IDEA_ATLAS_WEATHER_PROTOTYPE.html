Atlas outdoor weather

Rain and snow fall everywhere outside the floor plates and never over rooms. It runs while the rain sensor reads wet, or while the weather entity reports precipitation when there is no rain sensor. The rain sensor alone only ever shows light rain. It turns heavy only when an outside source confirms it: a rainfall or snowfall warning, or the weather entity reporting "pouring". It can be switched off in Atlas settings. Free users see it as a still texture; Pro animates it. Everything here is sample data.

Look into your house in 3D, like looking into a house in The Sims

Live Aboard builds your house in 3D from the map you already drew, with its floors, rooms, walls, doors, windows, lights and sensors. Turn it and zoom with your fingers, and double-tap a room to fly to it.

Since December 1, 2026, the 3D house is free, with no key: turn, pan and zoom round its floors, rooms, walls and lights, and Walk through it. Pro adds the weather outside, the view flying to what happens, and furniture and people (below).

  • Lit as it is now. Lights glow in their real colour and brightness. At night the house darkens with the real sun, and with the walls up the windows of lit rooms glow warm from outside.
  • The weather outside. Rain and snow fall outside the walls when your weather says so, and heavy snow builds a white layer. A window left open in the rain glows blue.
  • Studio. The house sits on a stage under a sky that follows the sun, through the day, golden hour and dusk to a night with stars.
  • Walk. Press Walk and you stand inside the front door at eye height. Use W A S D, or the stick on a touch screen, and drag to look round. Walls and closed doors stop you, a door with a sensor opens when it's open, and the stairs take you up and down.
  • It flies to what happens. On the sidebar and on wall screens, the view flies to a doorbell that rings, a door that opens or an alarm that goes off. It shows that thing's card, with a camera's snapshot if you pick one, and goes back after 20 seconds. You choose what it watches under Fly to when it goes off in Live Aboard's settings.
  • It's still the control panel. Tap a light to switch it, hold it for brightness and colour, and tap a room's name for its devices and scenes.

Turn a screen on the wall into the control panel for the house

Pro adds what a screen that runs all day needs: Dim at night between sunset and sunrise, Protect against burn-in for OLED screens, Show each floor to show the floors in turn, and On Fully Kiosk tablets, where someone walking up wakes a sleeping screen.

Open the Atlas as /padspan-lights?kiosk=1 and the screen becomes a wall. The whole house fits, every floor top to bottom, in landscape or portrait. In Settings → Atlas → 📺 Wall screens, give the screen's size and how far away people stand, and PadSpan sizes the text for that distance, so a 55-inch screen across a room gets far bigger text than a desk monitor. Furnish the 3D house

Mapping → Furnish adds furniture, devices and people to Live Aboard. Build ▾ has 33 kinds of furniture and devices, each with its own sliders, like a sofa's seats and arms, a bed's size or a table's shape. They're simple shapes, in the Sims' spirit. Room sets furnishes a whole kitchen, bathroom, bedroom, living room, dining room or office in one tap, along the walls and around the doors and windows, and one Undo takes the set away again.

  • Stairs. Straight, L or U, open or closed, with rails, and a hole cut in the floor above.
  • Floors and walls. Wood, tile, stone, carpet or concrete under each room, a colour for its walls, one feature wall, and the colour of each floor's outside walls.
  • Devices that come alive. Link a piece to Home Assistant and a lamp glows in its light's colour, the TV lights up while it plays, fan blades turn, the washer shakes while it runs and the robot vacuum leaves its dock.
  • People. Each person in Home Assistant as a figure you make with sliders for height, build, hair and colours, carrying the phones and tags you pick.
  • From elsewhere. A shared furniture library, off until you turn it on, and doors, windows and furniture from a Sweet Home 3D file.

Every add-on comes with Pro

PadSpan also sells add-ons, one feature each, for installs without Pro. Pro includes all of them, so a Pro key never needs one. The add-ons page has the details of each.

  • Audio. Each room's players in its card on the map, with mute, volume and off, a speaker beside a playing room's name, and Room off for the room's lights and players at once. You match each player to its room once, Control4, Crestron, Savant and Russound systems included.
  • Alarm & security. Arm and disarm your Home Assistant alarm from the Atlas's emergency button, open and close garage doors and gates from their card, with the lock beside them, and run the emergency lights test.
  • Climate & air quality. Each room coloured by its temperature, humidity or CO₂, from its own sensors, and each room's thermostats in its card, with the mode and the setpoint's − and +.
  • Vacation Mode & presets. Vacation Mode learns each light's routine from the house's own history and keeps it going while you're away. Whole house presets put every light and fan back the way you saved them.
  • Advanced smart lighting. Power teams that make a wall dimmer and its smart bulbs one light that follows the day, dims for sleep and wakes you, with WLED's Advanced controls, Automorph, Fit room, Light spread and fixture shapes with their size and angle on the Atlas.
  • Wi-Fi coverage. How strongly each access point reaches each part of the house, from UniFi Utopia's survey of your UniFi access points.
  • Looks pack. The Graphite, Ocean, Dusk and Day themes, Showcase's 20 map looks, including the West Coast Forest with trees round the house, and saved look presets.
  • Full house activity (listed as Time travel in PadSpan 0.38.300 and 0.38.301). The day replayed in Traceback on your Atlas map, with every light, door, lock and sensor and who was where, up to a week back. It's in Traceback, not on the Atlas page or a wall screen.
  • Presence insights. Locate gives room-by-room directions to a tag or phone from where your own phone is, Busy Times shows the busiest rooms by hour, and Forensics shows which Bluetooth devices were near a receiver in any time window, with CSV export and the recordings kept on your Home Assistant. It isn't Traceback's Insights tab, which is free.
  • Advanced map creation. AI plan reading, reading a photo, and floor plans, doors, windows, furniture and stairs from a LiDAR scan.
  • Robot vacuum. Your robot vacuum's map as a floor plan, the robot on the Atlas and in 3D with its card, room location runs, the mistake finder and Wi-Fi while it cleans.

One key covers all of it

Pro is the one thing to buy. It includes every add-on above, and every new PadSpan feature is included while the key runs. Start with the free map, add Pro when you want the rest, and make PadSpan as simple or complex as you need. Buying Pro also pays for the work on PadSpan, which gets new releases every week.

  • Up to five homes. One key covers up to five Home Assistants, such as your house, a cabin and a parent's house. Homes 1 and 2 always count. With more than two, every home on the key keeps its anonymous usage report on.
  • Homes come and go. A home that hasn't checked in for 45 days frees its place, and a reinstalled Home Assistant can take the place of one not seen for 14 days. Show this key's homes on the licence card lists them.
  • Same install. The key arrives by email when you pay. Enter it in Settings → Features → PadSpan licence and the Pro parts switch on, with nothing to reinstall.
  • Works without the internet. PadSpan checks the key once a day. When it can't reach the licence server it skips that check and carries on.

Questions

Do I install anything else for Pro?

No. Pro is a key for the PadSpan you already run. Enter it and the Pro parts switch on in the same install.

Does Pro need Bluetooth scanners?

Only for people and tags on the map. Placement, the 3D house, Furnish, the wall screenFixture shapes, Furnish, the wall screen's extras and the Atlas as Overview work from your floor plan and the devices Home Assistant already has.

Does the usage report have to stay on?

For the trial, a tester's key, an invite's free year, and a key used in more than two homes. A bought key in one or two homes doesn't need it. The report is anonymous counts of what is used and what breaks, and Settings shows exactly what it sends.

Is Live Aboard finished?

It's marked experimental, so it's still changing, and it stays off until you turn it on.It's no longer marked experimental, and it keeps getting better with each release. If something in your house looks wrong in 3D, please open an issue.

Which version of PadSpan do I need?

PadSpan needs Home Assistant 2024.8 or newer, and Pro works on the current stable release. Walk, floors and walls in Furnish, Audio, arming the alarm and the add-ons need the latest PadSpan.

Can I see it before I install?

Yes. The demo is the real PadSpan panel on a sample house, in your browser, and it runs as PadSpan Pro. Open the 3D house, the Atlas or the wall screen.

I only want one thing. Do I need Pro?

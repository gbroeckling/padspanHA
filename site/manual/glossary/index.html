A. Glossary

Contents

The words this manual and PadSpan's panel use, in alphabetical order.

Administrator. A Home Assistant user with administrator rights. Backups, restores, a Factory Reset, phones' keys, floor plan uploads and anything that sends data out of the house need one; with Enhanced security on, so does every change to PadSpan's shared setup.

Advertisement. A short Bluetooth message a phone, tag or beacon sends out over and over. Receivers hear advertisements; PadSpan never connects to the device.

Advanced, Basic and Development. The panel's three modes, switched by the mode button at the top right. Every screen is in the menu in every mode; the mode decides which are in the main list (the rest are under More tools) and how much detail each screen shows. Settings has only Appearance in Basic.

Area. A Home Assistant area (Settings → Areas, labels & zones). Normally a room in PadSpan is a Home Assistant area, and its name is the room's name everywhere. (Set to Standalone, in Settings → Tracking → Home Assistant and integrations → Room and receiver layout source, PadSpan uses rooms drawn in PadSpan and no areas at all.)

Atlas. PadSpan's map of the house for everyday light control, in the sidebar as Atlas, built in Mapping → Atlas builder.

Away timeout. How long a device goes unheard before it reads not_home (5 minutes unless you change it, in Settings → Tracking → Rooms and timing → Home/Away Timeout).

Backup. Here, either PadSpan's own (Manage → Data → Backup & Restore, kept on the machine) or Home Assistant's (Settings → System → Backups, which carries PadSpan's files). See chapter 22.

Beacon. A small device that only advertises: a tag, an iBeacon, a Find My tag. A pinned beacon is one you placed on the map at a fixed spot (Calibration → Beacon Tune), which can add calibration points by itself.

BLE. Bluetooth Low Energy, the kind of Bluetooth phones, watches and tags advertise with.

BLE Transmitter. A sensor in the Android Home Assistant Companion app that makes the phone send an iBeacon with a fixed id, so PadSpan can follow it without a key.

Bluetooth proxy, scanner. Other names for a receiver. "Bluetooth proxy" is ESPHome's and Home Assistant's word; older parts of PadSpan say "scanner".

Calibration point. A spot in a room where you stood with a device while PadSpan recorded what every receiver heard, and how loudly. With enough of them PadSpan can tell rooms apart that sound alike to the nearest receiver. Made in Calibration (🧭 Guided calibration, Pin & Listen, Beacon Tune, or Walk a room).

Commit this layout. The button in Mapping → Layout that puts the rooms and receivers drawn on a floor plan onto the map of the house, as Put it on the map does.

Consensus. The room most of the recent polls agree on. A new room has to win a vote of several polls (set by Room Change Delay) before a device moves there, so one loud reading doesn't move it. Receivers are judged against it in Health ("Agreement").

dBm. The unit of signal strength (RSSI). Closer to zero is louder: -55 dBm is near, -90 dBm far.

Device. Here, anything Bluetooth PadSpan hears: a phone, a watch, a tag, a beacon, a neighbour's earbuds. Also Home Assistant's word for one thing with its entities.

Diagnostics. PadSpan's report for support (QA → Go to Diagnostics). Home Assistant's own Download diagnostics is a different, much shorter report. See chapter 27.

Edition. Which download is installed: PadSpan HA (presence, maps and the Atlas) or PadSpan Bright (the Atlas alone, for light control without presence).

Enhanced security. Settings → Tracking → Privacy, updates and feedback → Enhanced security. When on, only an administrator can change PadSpan's shared setup; everyone can still view and use it. On for a new install.

Entity. One thing Home Assistant can show and automate: a tracker, a sensor, a light. PadSpan's are listed in chapter 14.

Export for support. Monitor → Export for support: the Diagnostics report plus what the receivers hear right now.

Fabric. PadSpan's map of the house in metres: each floor, its rooms' outlines, its walls, where each receiver and light is. Floor plan pictures are placed over it; positions are always worked out in it, never from the picture. Health calls it the Room and receiver layout.

Factory Reset. Manage → Factory Reset: empties PadSpan's files, including its own backups, and starts it as new. It keeps your licence key.

Find My tag. An AirTag or another tag that works with Apple's Find My. It changes its address, every 15 minutes near its owner and once a day away.

Fingerprint. What every receiver heard at a calibration point. PadSpan matches a device's live readings to the nearest fingerprints.

Floor. A Home Assistant floor (Settings → Areas, labels & zones). Each floor can have floor plans and its own height.

Floor plan. A picture of a floor, uploaded in Mapping (or made from a blank floor, a phone's room scan or a robot vacuum's map). Once its scale is set (a measured length on it), things drawn on it can go onto the map of the house.

Follow, followed. Following a device (on the Follow screen) watches it more closely: alerts when it changes room, arrives or leaves, and, with Room-change tag events on, the padspan_room_change event.

HACS. The Home Assistant Community Store, through which PadSpan is installed and updated on Home Assistant 2026.10 and older.

Health. The screen that gathers PadSpan's checks; the status dot opens it. Problems found is its list of things that can put devices in the wrong room.

iBeacon. A beacon format with a fixed UUID, major and minor number. PadSpan follows an iBeacon by those, not by its address.

IRK (Identity Resolving Key). A phone's or watch's secret key for its changing Bluetooth addresses. With it, PadSpan can tell that all those addresses are the same phone. Added in Bluetooth → IRK Manager, or in Home Assistant's Private BLE Device. PadSpan never sends one anywhere and hides them in every report.

Label, named device. A device you have given a name in PadSpan. Only named devices get Home Assistant entities, events and alerts.

Licence key. What unlocks PadSpan Pro (or Bright Pro), entered in Settings → Features → PadSpan licence. One key covers up to five homes.

Live Aboard. The Atlas in 3D (Pro, experimental, off unless switched on), with Furnish for furniture, devices and people.

Live and Sample. Live is your own house; Sample is a finished sample house to look around in (Preview a finished house, and Back to my house returns).

Lost & Found. The last room PadSpan saw each device in, kept so a device never goes back to "unknown".

Marketplace. Home Assistant's own store (Settings → Marketplace), from Home Assistant 2026.11.

More tools. The closed group at the bottom of the panel's menu with the screens that are not in the main list.

not_home. Home Assistant's word for away: a PadSpan tracker or area sensor reads not_home once the device has gone unheard for the away timeout.

Object history. PadSpan's list of every device it has heard. Unnamed ones drop out after Object History Retention (1 day unless you change it); named ones stay.

Poll. One round of PadSpan's room work, every 5 seconds unless you change it (Settings → Tracking → Performance → Presence Poll Interval).

Power team. A wall dimmer or switch and the smart bulbs it feeds, made into one light (Settings → Atlas → Power teams).

Private BLE Device. Home Assistant's integration that follows a phone by its IRK. PadSpan reads its keys; a key you add there reaches PadSpan within five minutes.

Pro, and the tiers. PadSpan HA is free with no key. A key's tier says what it unlocks: pro (PadSpan Pro: everything in PadSpan HA, such as Atlas placement, Live Aboard, wall mode, Vacation Mode, Forensics, Busy Times and Locate) or bright (Bright Pro: the Atlas's whole toolset). Free < Bright < Pro, each with all of the one before. There is a one-time 90-day free trial, in Settings → Features → PadSpan licence.

Put it on the map. The button (at the end of Setup, and on a room in Rooms) that takes rooms and receivers drawn on a measured floor plan onto the map of the house. Until then, devices are placed by nearest receiver only.

Receiver. A device that hears Bluetooth advertisements and passes them to Home Assistant: an ESPHome Bluetooth proxy, a Shelly Gen2 or newer, the Home Assistant machine's own Bluetooth, an ESPresense node or a Tasmota device. Also called a Bluetooth proxy or a scanner. One in each room you want told apart.

Room. A Home Assistant area (see Area), drawn on the map of the house. A receiver's area decides which room it stands in.

Room Change Delay. How long a new room must keep winning before a device moves there (40 seconds unless you change it).

Rotating address, random address. A Bluetooth address a phone, watch or Find My tag changes every so often so it can't be followed. PadSpan follows a phone through it with the phone's IRK or BLE Transmitter.

RSSI. Received signal strength: how loudly a receiver hears a device, in dBm.

Status dot. The small mark in the top bar that shows only when something is wrong: amber needs a look, red is broken.

Sub-zone. An area inside a room (a bed, a couch, a desk), drawn in Mapping → Edit → Sub-zones, with its own entities.

Traceback. The screen that shows where devices were, back in time.

Usage report. The opt-in, daily, anonymous report (Settings → Tracking → Help improve PadSpan). Off unless you turn it on.

Update check. PadSpan's daily question to padspan.traks.ca about a newer stable version, carrying only the version number.

Vacation Mode. Switches lights as on an ordinary day, learned from Home Assistant's history, while you are away (Mapping → Atlas builder, Pro).

Wall screen, kiosk. A tablet or screen on the wall showing the Atlas, opened with ?kiosk=1 (Settings → Atlas → Wall screens).

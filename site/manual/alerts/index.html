PadSpan sends to your phone or email through Home Assistant's own notify services. For a phone, that is the Home Assistant Companion app. For email, a mail integration such as SMTP. Set them up in Home Assistant first, under Settings → Devices & services.

Movement alerts (Follow)

Tell you when a device moves from room to room, arrives home, or leaves.

  1. Open Follow and pick the device under Choose a tag to follow.
  2. Under the device's card, find Movement Alerts.
  3. Choose where alerts go:
    • Your phone: pick it under Notify service (the Companion app's notify target). No email is needed.
    • Email: type the address under Email address, and leave Notify service on Auto-detect, or pick your mail service.
  4. Tick what you want to hear about. In Basic: Alert me when … moves, … arrives home, … leaves. In Advanced: Alert on every room change for "…", Alert when "…" arrives home, Alert when "…" leaves, and Only alert for these rooms (unchecked = all): to watch only some rooms (an alert then comes when the device goes into one of them).
  5. Choose Save. Nothing is active until you save it; the card then shows Active.
  6. Send Test sends one now, to exactly where alerts go, and says plainly if it couldn't reach your phone or mail service.

What the messages say:

  • PadSpan: Alex's phone moved: "Alex's phone moved from Kitchen to Hall".
  • PadSpan: Alex's phone arrived: "Alex's phone arrived (Kitchen)".
  • When it leaves: "Alex's phone left".

Some rules:

  • At most one room-change alert a minute for each device. A move inside that minute isn't lost: when the minute is up, one alert says where it went, from the room last reported.
  • Arrive and leave use the same away timeout as the Away badge, 5 minutes unless you change it in Settings → Tracking → Rooms and timing → Home/Away Timeout.
  • No alert after a restart. A device's first room after Home Assistant starts isn't a move.
  • A guest tag that is lent sends its stay's notices instead (below), not these.

Manage → Events has the same alerts for every device in one place: Saved Alerts lists each one, with a delete button, and Configure Alerts sets up room-change alerts for any tracked device, with Test Notification.

Arrive, leave and room changes also fire Home Assistant events (padspan_device_arrived, padspan_device_departed, padspan_room_change), for your own automations: see chapter 14.

No alert arrives? Use Send Test first: it says what went wrong. If no notify service is listed at all, add the Companion app (for a phone) or a mail integration in Home Assistant. More in chapter 26.

Leak, smoke, gas and CO alarms: the emergency latch

PadSpan watches every Home Assistant binary sensor of the kinds moisture (a water leak), smoke, gas and carbon monoxide, placed on the Atlas or not.

The moment one reports an alarm:

  • The alarm latches. It stays alarming in PadSpan for 2 days, or until someone resets it, even if the sensor itself goes quiet again. A short burst of wet is still a real leak.
  • A red banner (🚨 and how many are active) shows on every PadSpan screen, not just the Atlas, with each alarm's name and a Reset button. On a phone it is a small 🚨 pill in the top bar.
  • On the Atlas, the sensor's room rings red. The sensor says what it found (WET, SMOKE, GAS, CO), and ALARM while latched.

Reset asks once more (Yes / No), so a stray tap can't dismiss a real alarm. Resetting only clears PadSpan's memory of the alarm; it never touches the sensor's real state. A sensor that is still alarming stays red on the Atlas, and the banner comes back the next time it goes into alarm.

While the sample house is showing, the banner still shows your own house's alarms, with Back to my house in place of Reset.

PadSpan doesn't send a leak or smoke alarm to your phone. Use a Home Assistant automation on the sensor itself for that: it doesn't depend on PadSpan.

Alert pulse (Atlas)

Off until you turn it on: Settings → Atlas → Atlas — Mapped Light Control → Alert pulse. It saves as soon as you tick it.

With it on, the Atlas rings a room in amber when:

  • a window or cover (a blind, shade, shutter, awning) is open while it rains or snows (from the Atlas's own weather; see the Atlas guide);
  • a door, window, garage door, gate or cover the Atlas shows has been left open for more than 6 hours.

On a wall screen (?kiosk=1), a banner across the top names the worst alert and where it is ("Leak · Laundry", "Window open in the rain · Bedroom"), with how many more, until it clears. The order, worst first: smoke, gas, CO, a leak, an opening in the rain, a door left open.

Vacation Mode notices

Vacation Mode (PadSpan Pro) runs your lights while you're away (Atlas guide). It tells you when it stops:

  • PadSpan: Vacation Mode ended: it reached the end date set for it.
  • PadSpan: Vacation Mode turned off: someone turned it off. The message says when, who, and from which app or browser: "Vacation Mode was turned off Nov 21, 18:40 by Alex from the iPhone app. If nobody meant to, turn it back on in Whole House Presets: within 3 weeks it carries on with the routine it was using." A Factory Reset says so too.

The notice is a Home Assistant notification, and goes as well to the phones and email addresses your Follow alerts use (up to five).

binary_sensor.padspan_vacation_mode and the padspan_ha_vacation_mode event are there for your own automations.

Guest notices

When you lend a guest tag (Guests → Lend to a guest; guest tags are on in Settings → Tracking → Phones and guest tags), the stay has its own notices:

NoticeWhen
PadSpan: Sam arrivedThe tag is heard in the house, if Tell me when they arrive is ticked.
PadSpan: Sam leftThe tag is no longer heard, if Tell me when they leave is ticked. A low battery can look the same as leaving.
PadSpan: Sam in the OfficeThe guest is in a room you set as off-limits (Rooms they shouldn't go into) for half a minute.
PadSpan: is Sam's stay over?The tag hasn't been heard for two days.
PadSpan: Sam's stay endedAt the stay's planned end, or after a week without the tag being heard.
  • Tell me when they arrive and Tell me when they leave start ticked.
  • Send notices to: a phone or other notify target, or A Home Assistant notification (the default).
  • Quiet hours: no arrive or leave notices then. Off-limits notices still come.

Every one of these also fires the padspan_ha_guest event. The Guest tags guide has the rest.

Repairs

PadSpan raises one kind of Home Assistant repair, under Settings → Repairs:

PadSpan could not confirm a save (…): PadSpan saved its data, but the file on disk does not hold what was saved, or could not be read back.

The latest changes may then be only in memory, and lost at the next restart. Check that the disk Home Assistant runs on isn't full or read-only, and look for the error in Home Assistant's log. The notice goes away by itself the next time a save is confirmed. It is kept over a restart.

Other Home Assistant notifications

  • PadSpan HA update available, once for each new stable version. To turn it off: Settings → Tracking → Privacy, updates and feedback → Update Check. See chapter 3.
  • PadSpan: AI credit, once a month, when Claude's reads by PadSpan pass the share you chose of the monthly credit you typed in Settings → AI (Your AI: Warn me). Off unless you set both.
  • padspan_ha.dump_devices: the list of named devices, when you call that service yourself.

Inside the panel only

These show in PadSpan and send nothing:

  • The status dot in the top bar, amber or red when something needs a look. See chapter 12.
  • Health → Problems found: the checks of your whole setup.
  • Calibration Accuracy Reminders (Settings → Tracking → Positioning and calibration, off by default): a reminder in the panel when calibration has gaps or is getting old.

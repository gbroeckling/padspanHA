25. PadSpan Pro: trial, keys, homes, testers, invites

Contents

PadSpan HA is free, and the presence tracking stays free: rooms, floor plans, calibration, the history, the Home Assistant sensors and the alerts. So are, from December 1, 2026, exact placement of lights and devices on the Atlas, the plain full-screen wall screen and the 3D house's basics (Live Aboard). PadSpan Pro is an optional key that unlocks the paid parts on the same install, with nothing to reinstall:

  • on the Atlas: shapes, sizes and angles, Fit room, WLED's Advanced controls, Automorph and Showcase (chapter 15);
  • power teams: making and changing them, following the day, Sleep and the wake-up (chapter 15);
  • in Live Aboard, the 3D house: Furnish, people figures, the 3D editor, the director and the library (chapter 16, chapter 17);
  • on a wall screen: night dimming, the burn-in shift, floors in turn and Fully Kiosk motion wake (chapter 18);
  • Whole house presets and Vacation Mode;
  • Forensics, Busy Times and Locate (chapter 12);
  • the AI features (chapter 19);
  • from December 1, 2026, the robot vacuum's features (chapter 9);
  • anything gated later.

Until December 1, 2026, exact placement, wall mode and the whole 3D house are Pro's too.

A PadSpan Pro key also unlocks PadSpan Bright's tools. Prices and where to buy a key: padspan.traks.ca. Without Pro, an add-on unlocks one feature (Add-ons).

Everything below is in Settings → Features → PadSpan licence unless it says otherwise. Starting a trial, entering a key and signing up as a tester need a Home Assistant administrator.

Try it free for 90 days

Start 90-day free trial (on the licence card, and on the trial card the Overview shows once PadSpan is working in your house) gives you all of Pro for 90 days. No card, one trial per home, and your email is used only to send your key.

The trial needs the usage report on (Settings → Tracking → Help improve PadSpan: anonymous counts of what is used and what breaks, chapter 24). With it off, the trial card asks you to turn it on in the same step. The usage report stays on for the whole trial, and can't be turned off until the trial ends or you enter a bought key.

When the trial ends, nothing you set up is deleted. It all comes back as you left it when you buy.

Enter a key

Choose Enter licence key and paste it (PSPAN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). The card says Licence activated. Replace licence key swaps it; Show licence key shows it (administrators only). Once a day PadSpan checks the key, so a refunded or revoked key stops working; what that check sends is in chapter 24.

One key, five homes

One key covers up to five Home Assistants (a cabin, a parent's house, a test install):

  • Homes 1 and 2 always count.
  • With more than two homes, every home on the key must keep its usage report on, the first two included. A third home can join only when every home, the new one too, has the report on.
  • If one of them turns the report off, the key drops back to its first two homes after 14 days. The first two keep Pro; the others pick up again at their next check once the report is back on. The 14 days add up: turning the report back on briefly doesn't restart them, and they start again only after every home has had the report on for 30 days. The licence card says which home has it off and by when, and turning the report off on such a key asks first.
  • A home on PadSpan before 0.38.160 can't send the report, so it counts as off until it is updated; the licence card names it.
  • A home that hasn't checked in for 45 days frees its place, and a new or reinstalled Home Assistant can take the place of a home not seen for 14 days.

Show this key's homes lists them, lets you name this one and remove one you no longer use. If a home isn't counted, the licence card says why.

Advanced map creation and Robot vacuum are on sale from December 1, 2026. A floor plan from a LiDAR scan and the robot vacuum's features are free until then; from then they need PadSpan Pro or their add-on (a Bright Pro key keeps the robot vacuum's). Whoever holds one of the two pays 30% less for the other: buy it from the licence card, which links to the payment at that price, and pay with the email the key was sent to. The better of that and any sale applies, never both.

Free on every install, with or without them: an alarm going off (in the emergency button, with nothing to press, and in the red banner), locking and unlocking on the map and in a room's card, Alert pulse, the Insights tab, a device's 📍 Locate flash, and a power team already made, as one light (on, off, dimming, its power and each bulb).

  • Your add-ons share one key: buy another with the same email and it joins that key. Enter it here like any key; the licence card lists each add-on with its date. An add-on key is not a Pro key.
  • Each add-on gets the same 14 days past its date that a key does.
  • The 90-day trial is still yours: it keeps the add-on key aside and puts it back when it ends.
  • Where a feature is locked, it says what unlocks it: "PadSpan Pro or the … add-on", with the price.
  • A key holding only add-ons newer than your PadSpan still reads as an add-on key, never as Pro: the licence card says "This add-on needs a newer PadSpan — update PadSpan." Update, and it works.

Get Pro free by testing it

Become a tester (on the licence card, or under Settings → Tracking → Help improve PadSpan) gives you two years of PadSpan Pro free (one year if you join from December 16, 2026), for people happy to be contacted about trying new things before they ship. The usage report stays on for that time: it can't be turned off while the key runs. You give an email and can say what you'd like to test; you see exactly what goes before anything is sent, and Stop being a tester withdraws your sign-up at any time. What is sent and kept: chapter 24.

Invites

Now and then, an install that sends the usage report is invited to a free year of PadSpan Pro. It shows on the Overview and on the licence card, to administrators only. The report stays on for the free year.

  • Claim my free year: type an email and the key arrives there. No card, nothing renews.
  • Later: asks again in a week.
  • No thanks: final; it won't come back.

Nothing is sent unless you press one of those.

Wall screens

A screen opened with ?kiosk=1 never shows trial or invite offers (chapter 18).

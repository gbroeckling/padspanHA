27. Getting help

Contents

Before asking, chapter 26 has the common problems by symptom. When you do ask, a report from PadSpan saves a round of questions. There are three, and they hold different things.

The three reports

ReportWhereHolds
Diagnostics (PadSpan's)QA → Go to Diagnostics (QA is under More tools)Versions, the panel's timings, PadSpan's status, your floor plans' names, and your own devices: the ones you named, linked to Home Assistant, or phones known by their key, heard in the last 24 hours. Download Report saves it as a file; Copy copies it.
Export for supportMonitor → Export for support (Monitor is under More tools)The same, plus the live view as PadSpan sees it now: devices and advertisements, cut to 200 of each (all of your own devices are kept), and the receivers. Build export, then Copy.
Download diagnostics (Home Assistant's)Settings → Devices & services → PadSpan HA → ⋮ → Download diagnosticsLittle: PadSpan's version, its config entry, its status and the saved room list (room_tag_map). Not your devices, rooms or receivers.

For most problems, PadSpan's Diagnostics is the one to send. For a device in the wrong room or not showing at all, Export for support shows what the receivers hear.

What they leave out, and what they don't

  • Phones' identity keys (IRKs) are never in them. Each one is replaced by irk:hidden-1, irk:hidden-2 and so on, the same number for the same phone throughout, so the report still makes sense.
  • They do name your rooms, floor plans and devices, with their Bluetooth addresses. That is what makes them useful, and it is why you should look a report over before you send it anywhere public.
  • Errors PadSpan handled quietly (on the Diagnostics screen, for administrators) are not in the report: an error's text can name your rooms and devices. If support asks for them, copy the lines you are happy to share.
  • Nothing is sent by any of them. They only make text for you to copy or save.

Logs

PadSpan's own log is in Manage → Logs. For Home Assistant's log with more detail from PadSpan, turn on Enable debug logging on PadSpan HA's page in Settings → Devices & services, reproduce the problem, then turn it off: Home Assistant offers the log as a file when you do.

Where to ask

  • A bug: open an issue at github.com/gbroeckling/padspanHA/issues and choose Bug Report. Issues are public, so remove what you'd rather not show (names, addresses) from anything you attach.
  • By email: hello@traks.ca. An email is not public, so a report can go as it is.
  • A security problem: not in a public issue. See SECURITY.md.
  • An idea: the Feature request form in the same issues list.

What to include

  1. PadSpan's version and Home Assistant's. PadSpan's is under its name at the top of the panel's menu, and in every report. Home Assistant's is in Settings → About.
  2. How PadSpan was installed: the Marketplace, HACS or by hand.
  3. Your receivers: what kind (ESPHome Bluetooth proxy, Shelly, Home Assistant's own Bluetooth, ESPresense, Tasmota) and how many.
  4. What happened, and what you expected. For a room problem: which device, which room it showed, and which room it was really in, with the time.
  5. The steps that make it happen again, if you know them.
  6. A report (above), and a screenshot of the screen in question.
  7. Anything that changed just before: an update, a new receiver, a moved receiver, new calibration.

A wrong-room report is most useful taken while it is wrong: build Export for support then, before the device moves on.

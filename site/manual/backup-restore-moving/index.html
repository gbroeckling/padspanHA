22. Backup, restore, factory reset, moving to a new Home Assistant

Contents

PadSpan keeps two kinds of copy of your house, and they do different jobs.

  • PadSpan's own backups (Manage → Data → Backup & Restore) are for going back: before a big change, or after one that went wrong. They live inside PadSpan's own files, on the same machine.
  • Home Assistant's backup is for losing the machine, or moving to a new one. It carries PadSpan's files with the rest of Home Assistant.

You need both. A PadSpan backup does not survive the machine, and it does not survive a Factory Reset.

PadSpan's own backups

Open Manage → Data → Backup & Restore (Manage is under More tools in Basic mode). Only a Home Assistant administrator can make, restore or delete one.

What a backup holds

Create Backup takes a copy of these, all in one go:

  • the settings, including your licence key
  • the floor plans: their records and the pictures themselves
  • rooms, floors, walls, placed lights and receivers (the layout and the room model)
  • calibration points and the calibration model
  • your device names and PadSpan's list of devices
  • follow alerts
  • movement history, the last day of Traceback, and the last room of each device (Lost & Found)
  • the object history, and the Forensics sessions
  • what PadSpan remembers about each WLED device
  • Live Aboard's furniture and heights, sub-zones, the plan AI's suggestions and the robot vacuum link, each only if you have used it

Not in a PadSpan backup: guest tags and their stays, RSSI recordings, the AI use counter and the usage report's counters. Home Assistant's own areas, floors, entities and automations are never in it: they are Home Assistant's, in Home Assistant's backup.

Where backups live, and how many

All of PadSpan's backups are kept in one file, .storage/padspan_ha.backups, with the floor plan pictures inside it. PadSpan keeps the 3 newest backups you made (a fourth removes the oldest). Before some changes it takes one by itself ("Before merging rooms…", "Before putting receivers in their rooms…", "Before removing all furniture in Live Aboard", a PadSpan Bright import and others); it keeps the 3 newest of those apart, so they never push out one of yours. There is no button to download a backup: to keep one off the machine, use Home Assistant's backup, which carries this file.

Restore

  1. In Backup & Restore, choose Restore… on the backup.
  2. Tick what to bring back. Each part of the backup is ticked to start with, and Maps Config and Model go together (a plan and where it sits are one thing). The pictures are a tick of their own, Map Images.
  3. Choose Restore selected.
  4. Reload PadSpan (Settings → Devices & services → PadSpan HA → ⋮ → Reload) for everything to take effect, as the message says.

A restore puts back what you ticked, with a few things kept as they are now, because they belong to this install and not to the house:

  • Your licence: a backup from before you bought a key, or from a trial, never takes the key away. The better licence of the two stays.
  • A tester sign-up, an invite's answer, and what the update check last said.
  • Movement and Lost & Found are joined with what happened since, not replaced.
  • Live Aboard's furniture placed since stays when the backup has none.

Factory Reset

Manage → Factory Reset empties PadSpan's files and starts it again as new, with PadSpan still installed. It works only in Live mode, needs an administrator, and asks you to type FACTORY RESET before Erase All Data & Reset can be pressed.

It erases calibration, floor plans and their pictures, rooms and receivers, your device names, follow alerts, history and Traceback, Forensics, learned data, Live Aboard's furniture, guest tags, sub-zones, every setting, and all of PadSpan's own backups. Make a Home Assistant backup first if you might want any of it back.

It keeps your licence key, a tester sign-up and furniture you shared to the library (so you can still withdraw either), and Vacation Mode's learned routine. It changes nothing in Home Assistant itself: areas, floors, entities and devices stay. A Factory Reset during a vacation turns Vacation Mode off.

Home Assistant's backup

Home Assistant's own backup (Settings → System → Backups) copies its config folder, and that is where PadSpan keeps everything:

WhatWhere in the config folder
PadSpan's data, settings and its own backups.storage/padspan_ha.*
Traceback's history, and RSSI recordings.storage/padspan_ha.traceback_segments/ and .storage/padspan_ha.capture_sessions/
Floor plan picturespadspan_ha/maps/
WLED backups made from PadSpanpadspan_ha/wled_backups/
PadSpan itselfcustom_components/padspan_ha/

So every Home Assistant backup has all of PadSpan in it. From Home Assistant 2025.1, "Home Assistant settings" (the config folder) is in every backup and can't be left out; what you choose is whether to add the history database, media, the share folder and add-ons. PadSpan needs none of those. On an older Home Assistant OS, a partial backup holds PadSpan only if Home Assistant is ticked in it.

The history database is not PadSpan's: it holds the past states of PadSpan's entities, as it does for every entity. Leaving it out loses that history, nothing else.

Moving to a new Home Assistant machine

The way that carries everything is Home Assistant's own:

  1. On the old machine, make a backup: Settings → System → Backups → Back up now. Download it, and keep its encryption key.
  2. Switch the old machine off, or at least stop Home Assistant on it. Two machines running the same house both listen to the same receivers and both run the same automations, and PadSpan's licence check sees two homes (below).
  3. On the new machine, start Home Assistant's onboarding and choose to restore from a backup. Give it the file and the key.
  4. When Home Assistant is up, open PadSpan. Your house, plans, calibration, names and settings are as they were.

Then check these, which depend on the machine rather than on the files:

  • The new machine's own Bluetooth. Receivers on the network (ESPHome proxies, Shelly devices, ESPresense, Tasmota) are the same receivers and carry on. If the old machine's own Bluetooth adapter was a receiver, the new one is a different receiver: the old one shows as not heard, and the new one as new. Place the new one on the map, and use Replace Scanner (Settings → Scanner Map) to move the old one's calibration to it.
  • Its memory. Next to a busy street PadSpan hears thousands of devices. See Minimum requirements before moving to a 2 GB machine.
  • Your licence. The restored copy carries the old machine's place among your licence's homes. The licence server hands that place over when the old machine has stopped checking in. If both keep running, the new one has to take a home of its own.
  • Wall screens and links: if the new machine has a new address, open each wall screen's link again with the new address.

Copying only PadSpan's files (the folders above) into a Home Assistant set up from scratch is not the same: PadSpan's rooms and layout point at Home Assistant's floors and areas, and its devices at what Home Assistant knows. It works only where the new Home Assistant has the same floors and areas. A full Home Assistant backup brings those too.

Coming from PadSpan Bright

PadSpan Bright (the lighting-only edition) and PadSpan HA can be installed side by side, and PadSpan HA can take the house you built in Bright.

  1. Install PadSpan HA (chapter 3), keeping Bright installed.
  2. In PadSpan HA, open Health. A card PadSpan Bright found on this Home Assistant says what Bright holds.
  3. Choose Import from PadSpan Bright. PadSpan HA takes a backup of its own first (it is in Backup & Restore), copies Bright's floors, rooms, walls, placed lights, floor plans and their pictures, settings and Live Aboard's furniture, then reloads.
  4. When the house looks right in PadSpan HA, remove PadSpan Bright. The import never deletes Bright's files.

The import runs only into an empty house: if PadSpan HA already has a floor, a room, a light, a wall or a map, the card says so and offers nothing, and it never merges two houses. Bright's licence key comes across with its settings, unless you have already entered a key in PadSpan HA.

19. AI features

Contents

PadSpan has no AI of its own and sends nothing to an AI service itself. A few features can ask an AI you have set up in Home Assistant, an AI Task, to read a picture for you: a photo of a sofa, or a floor plan. Room presence, calibration and everything else work without one.

What it can do

All of these need PadSpan Pro, and each read happens only when someone starts it, never on a schedule.

The experimental ones are research: the AI suggests, you check every item, and nothing is saved until you accept it. They never change your calibration or signal walls. What they can save (a plan's picture lined up, rooms) takes a backup first and has an undo. Turning a switch off hides the feature; it doesn't undo what you kept.

Ask your voice assistant where someone is is the one row of What it does for you marked COMING SOON: it can't be switched on yet. Assist's own "where is" sentences work today without an AI: chapter 14.

Connecting an AI

Settings → AI → Your AI:

  • AI Task: pick one whose model can read pictures (a vision model). It needs Home Assistant 2025.8 or newer. If Home Assistant has none yet, add an AI integration with a vision model first: one that runs in your home, such as Ollama, or a cloud one.
  • Backup AI task: used when the main one can't be reached (a computer that's away, say). Before each read, PadSpan opens and closes one connection to the main one's server to see whether it is there; nothing is sent on it.

None is picked by default, and with none picked nothing is read. Only an administrator picks one.

Connecting Claude

Build from plans and Let Claude draw your rooms need Claude, through Home Assistant's own Anthropic integration and your own Anthropic account. Anthropic charges that account for each read; PadSpan sells no credit and never asks for a card. It takes about 15 minutes, once.

  1. In the Anthropic Console: sign up or sign in, and add credit on the Billing page. If your Claude plan includes monthly API credit, link your Console organisation to it instead. A Claude subscription on its own doesn't pay for these reads.
  2. Open API keys, choose Create Key, name it ("Home Assistant") and copy it. The Console shows it only once.
  3. In Home Assistant: Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → Anthropic, paste the key and finish.
  4. In the Anthropic integration, open its AI Task (or add one), choose Claude Opus 5.5 or newer as its model by its full name, and save. Sonnet and Haiku aren't accepted: the plan reading was tested on Opus 5.5.
  5. In PadSpan: the helper's Connect Claude screen checks again by itself when you come back (or choose Check again). If Home Assistant has a usable Claude that isn't PadSpan's AI yet, it offers Use it for PadSpan, and your earlier AI Task stays as the backup. Or pick it yourself in Settings → AI → Your AI.

Before anything is read, the helper sends one tiny read with no plan in it (a blank one-pixel picture and the words "This is a connection check. Reply with the single word OK."), so an account with no credit is found then, not after you measured your plan.

When a read fails: out of credit, add credit and choose Try again (what was read is kept). Claude couldn't be reached: check the Anthropic integration in Settings → Devices & services and Home Assistant's log. The media folder couldn't take the plan: Home Assistant's media folder must be writable, because PadSpan hands the picture to the AI Task through it.

What is read, and where it goes

Every read is a call to Home Assistant's own ai_task.generate_data on the AI Task you picked, carrying:

  • one picture: the photo, or a floor plan (whole, or a crop of it). It goes as it is, so whatever is printed on a plan goes with it;
  • instructions written into PadSpan, which add only the picture's size in pixels and, for a photo, the kind of piece you picked;
  • for a photo, the list of fields to fill in.

PadSpan adds no names, addresses, people, devices or positions from your house. Home Assistant hands the read to the AI behind the AI Task: one that runs in your home keeps the picture there, and a cloud one sends it to that company, under its own terms. For the length of the call the picture is a file in a hidden folder in Home Assistant's media folder, removed when the call ends. PadSpan keeps no copy of a photo. One photo or plan can take several reads.

A person's figure from a photo asks first that the person agrees, sends nothing until that is ticked, and recommends a local AI Task.

What it costs

PadSpan costs nothing extra here: you pay the AI's maker, if anyone. A local AI (Ollama on your own machine) costs only its electricity. A cloud AI charges its own account.

For Claude, Settings → AI keeps a count: Every read PadSpan sent to your AI this month, with an estimated cost for Claude only.

  • It is an estimate from each picture's size and each question's length: Home Assistant doesn't pass on what Claude counted. The exact numbers are in the Anthropic Console → Usage, filtered by the key Home Assistant uses.
  • Type Your monthly credit, US$ and pick a share (50, 75 or 90%), and PadSpan sends one Home Assistant notification a month when the estimate passes it (PadSpan: AI credit).
  • The count stays on this Home Assistant: it isn't in backups, a factory reset empties it, and the usage report gets only counts and rough bands from it.

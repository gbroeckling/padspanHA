Appendix B. Every screen and tab

Contents

Every screen in PadSpan's menu, in the menu's order, with its tabs. The table is read from the panel's own code, so it names exactly what the panel shows in English. Which screens each mode (Basic, Advanced, More tools) shows is in chapter 12. Each screen's ? button explains it in the panel.

ScreenWhat it isIts tabs
OverviewWhere everyone and everything is now, on a drawing of your house; before any room is drawn, Who's where now.–
Live TrackerThe live map on its own, full screen, for a wall tablet or a second screen.–
FollowOne person or thing: its room now, its movements, Locate (Pro) and alerts when it moves.–
DevicesEvery device PadSpan tracks or hears, in four lists, and the device registry.–
BluetoothWhat each receiver hears, and the IRK Manager for phones and watches.Visualisation, Advertisements, Scanners, IRK Manager, ESPHome Configs
PresenceFor experts: which rooms each device has been seen in, and what each receiver hears.–
MonitorWhat PadSpan hears, in numbers, and Export for support.–
SetupThe steps from a floor plan to a calibrated map, in order.–
MappingFloor plans, their scale, rooms, receivers, floors, and the Atlas builder.Library, Upload, Edit, 3D Stack, Layout, Atlas builder, Export, Help, Furnish (with Live Aboard on)
RoomsEvery room's settings in one place: colour, outline, ceiling height, receivers, who is in it.–
ReceiversEvery receiver in one table: room, floor, height, signal adjustment, status.–
SettingsEvery setting, in tabs.Appearance, Scanner Map, Tracking, Features, AI, Atlas, UI Structure. Tracking is in sections: Rooms and timing, Phones and guest tags, What the panel shows, Positioning and calibration, Receivers, Performance, Home Assistant and integrations, Privacy, updates and feedback
ManageData clean-ups, backups, Home Assistant entities, alerts by email or phone, logs, Factory Reset.Data, HA Entities, Beacon Characteristics, History, Events, Logs, Factory Reset
TrainingAnimated walkthroughs and the Full Manual, inside the panel.–
CalibrationTeach PadSpan what each spot sounds like, and see how accurate it is.Results, Tune, Beacon Tune, Beacon and floor, Pin & Listen, Roam, Model, Accuracy by spot
TracebackReplay where devices went, with Insights (time in each room) and Busy Times (Pro).–
ForensicsPro: which Bluetooth devices were near your receivers between two times.–
OccupancyHow many people are in the building, and how the number was reached.–
GuestsGuest tags: who is staying now, the tags you lend, and past stays.–
HealthIs PadSpan working? Its own checks, receivers' health, and Problems found.–
QAChecks on your setup, backups, and each receiver ranked.–
SandboxA look under the hood at the live data; changes nothing.–
Install BaseThe developer's screen for the opt-in usage reports; asks for a PIN.–

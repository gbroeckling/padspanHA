17. Furnish

Contents

Furnish puts furniture, devices and people into Live Aboard, the 3D house (chapter 16). It needs PadSpan Pro and Live Aboard turned on, and it is in Mapping → Furnish: the tab appears next to the Atlas builder once Live Aboard is on, in Advanced mode. As in Edit, nothing is stored until you press Save, and Undo steps back through everything.

Adding a piece

Build ▾ adds a piece of furniture or a device: a sofa, a bed, a table, a lamp, a TV, stairs and more, or a plain Box. Each kind has its own sliders (a sofa's seats and arms, a bed's size, a table's shape). They are simple shapes in the Sims' spirit, not scans.

The other ways in:

  • From a photo: photograph a real piece and PadSpan builds a matching one. It needs Home Assistant 2025.8 or newer with an AI Task that can read images, picked in Settings → AI, where Furniture from a photo can also be switched off (chapter 19). With a cloud AI Task, the photo leaves your house to be read; it is never stored. It asks for one real measurement unless something of known size (a door, a tape measure) is in the photo. If the answer makes no sense, you get a box, never an error.
  • Library: furniture other PadSpan houses shared. Turn on Shared library in the Live Aboard settings and accept its terms first. Off, Live Aboard never contacts the library.
  • Import: doors, windows and furniture from a Sweet Home 3D file (.sh3d), or, with RoomPlan import on (Settings → Features), from a phone's room scan (.usdz or .json). Each door or window goes on the nearest wall; one with no wall near it is left out and counted. You see them first and pick what to keep.
  • People & devices: below.

Kitchens and bathrooms

Build ▾ has a Kitchen and a Bathroom heading:

  • Kitchen: base cabinets (doors, drawers, or a drawer over doors), wall cabinets, tall cabinets (with an oven, or an oven and a microwave), a sink with one or two bowls, a hob or a cooker, a dishwasher and a fridge. Each base unit has its own worktop, so a row of them reads as one.
  • Bathroom: a toilet, a basin (on a vanity, a pedestal or the wall), a bath and a shower.
  • A sink's or basin's Height goes up to the top of its tap: a 1.2 m sink has its worktop at 0.9 m, level with the cabinets beside it, and fits under a sill as high as its worktop.
  • To put up a wall cabinet, set its Height in room to 1.45 m (the usual height over a 0.9 m worktop) and drag it to the wall, or use Hang on wall.

Room sets

Build ▾ → Room sets furnishes a whole room in one tap: Kitchen, one wall, Kitchen, L-shaped, Bathroom, Bedroom, Living room, Dining room or Office.

  • Turn the view so the room is in the middle, then pick a set.
  • Pieces go along the walls, backs to them, stepping past doors, windows and anything already there, and always inside the room. PadSpan tries each wall and keeps the one that fits the most.
  • In a kitchen, the wall cabinets hang over the base units, and the sink goes under a window.
  • A piece with no room is left out, and the message says how many.
  • It is a first go to adjust. One Undo takes the whole set away.

Floors and walls

Tap a room's floor (with no piece picked) and its panel opens (for the kitchen, Kitchen: floor and walls). Tap another room to switch to it; tap outside the house to close it.

  • Floor: As drawn (the room's colour, as before), or a material: Wood, Light wood, Tile, Stone, Carpet or Concrete. Each is drawn with its own colour and its planks or joints, laid square to the room's longest wall.
  • Walls: the colour of the room's walls on the inside. The other side of a wall it shares keeps the next room's colour.
  • One wall a colour of its own: lists the room's walls by number and length; pointing at a row outlines that wall. Give one an accent colour.
  • Outside walls: the colour of the outside walls of the whole floor.
  • As drawn next to any colour takes it back to how it was.

Each change is one Undo, and nothing is kept until Save. The floors and walls are in Live Aboard's own file, so a PadSpan backup carries them and a restore brings them back.

They show in Live Aboard's own looks, Classic and Studio (Views ▾ → Look). A Showcase theme, and the Atlas's look, keep their own colours: there the house looks as it did. Until you pick something, nothing changes anywhere.

Moving and setting a piece

  • Drag a piece across the floor. Its back snaps to the walls, and two short lines show how far it is from the nearest walls.
  • Arrow keys nudge it 1 cm (Shift: 10 cm); [ and ] turn it 15° (Shift: 1°).
  • Its panel has its label, size, its builder's settings, colours, Turn, Floor ▲ / ▼, Height in room, exact X, Y, Height and Angle, Duplicate and Delete. Stand on what's under it puts a lamp on its table; Hang on wall at a height you pick.
  • Warnings tell you when a piece doesn't fit (through a wall, off the floor).

Stairs

Build ▾ → House → Stairs: which way they turn and the floor they go up to. Their height is the gap up to that floor, and they open a hole in it.

  • Shape: Straight, L, U, 2 landings or 3 landings (round an open well).
  • Style: Closed (solid steps), Open (treads on a board each side), Treads only (as if fixed into the wall) or Floating (treads on one steel spine).
  • Rail: None until you pick Both, Inside or Outside. Rail style: Balustrade, Topless or Single bar.
  • Stairs in stair rooms (shown when there are some): a first guess for each room called Stairs, Stairwell or Staircase that has none yet and a floor above, sized to the room.
  • Read stairs off the plans (AI) (with AI map-building research (experimental) and Stairs from your floor plans on in Settings → AI, and an AI Task picked): your AI looks for stairs on each floor plan and adds what it finds, unsaved. It is a first guess: check every one. A plan is sent to your AI only when you press it.
  • From a file: Import brings a Sweet Home 3D staircase in as stairs, and a RoomPlan scan's stairs too.

A piece that's a real device

This is a device… links a piece to a Home Assistant entity, and it behaves like it: a lamp glows in its light's colour, a TV lights up while playing, a fan's blades turn, a washer shakes while it runs, a robot vacuum leaves its dock while working, a radiator glows while heating. If the entity is renamed, the piece follows it; if it is deleted, the piece shows Unlinked and stays put.

People

People & devices shows each Home Assistant person in the house:

  • A figure made with sliders (height, build, hair, colours), or from a photo. A figure is a few simple shapes, never a likeness. The photo path asks first that the person agrees, and sends nothing until that is ticked. It recommends a local AI Task.
  • Carries: which phones and tags each person carries, from what PadSpan tracks. Those place the person in Live Aboard and on the Atlas.
  • People are never shared: figures live only in this house's 3D file. Deleting a person in Home Assistant takes their figure with it.

Show people and Show tags & scanners, in Settings or in Live Aboard's Views ▾, show or hide them.

Sharing a piece

Share… shares a piece you built to the library: only its shape, sizes, colours and the details you fill in, never a photo or anything about your house. A title with an email, phone number, address or link is refused. You can withdraw a share later.

Clearing it out

Settings → Atlas → Live Aboard has Remove all furniture… (administrators). A backup is taken first; to bring the furniture back, restore that backup (chapter 22).

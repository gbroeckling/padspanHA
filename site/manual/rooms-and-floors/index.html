8. Rooms and floors come from Home Assistant

Contents

PadSpan has no room list of its own. Its rooms are your Home Assistant areas, and its floors are your Home Assistant floors. You add, rename and remove them in Home Assistant, and PadSpan follows. What PadSpan keeps for each room is what Home Assistant has no place for: its outline on the floor plan, its colour, its ceiling height and the like.

Rooms are areas

  • A receiver's room is the area of its Home Assistant device. A receiver with no area gives no room, however well it hears you (chapter 5).
  • A device's room is the room of the receiver that hears it best, or, with three or more receivers on a floor plan, the area whose outline it is in.
  • The room PadSpan reports to Home Assistant (a tracker's state, an area sensor) is the area's name, written as Home Assistant has it: "Kitchen", not "kitchen".

So for rooms, every room you want told apart needs an area in Home Assistant, and a receiver in it with that area. A room you only want on the map (a closet, a hall) needs an area and an outline, but no receiver.

Add areas in Home Assistant: Settings → Areas, labels & zones. If Home Assistant has no areas yet, the Overview's Who's where now card says so.

Add one from PadSpan. When you draw rooms in Mapping → Edit and the plan's floor has no area left to choose, PadSpan says: "There is no room to choose for this floor yet: rooms are Home Assistant's areas." Type a name and choose Add to Home Assistant: Home Assistant gets an area of that name, on the plan's floor. A name that is already an area is used, never made twice. The room helper Claude can draw with (chapter 19) has the same offer.

Floors are Home Assistant floors

Give each area its floor in Home Assistant (an area's Floor, under Settings → Areas, labels & zones). PadSpan reads the floors and stacks them bottom-up:

  1. by each floor's level in Home Assistant, when it has one;
  2. otherwise by its name: basement and cellar go below, ground, main and first floor at ground level, outdoors at ground level too;
  3. otherwise above the named ones, in the order they were made.

Set a level on every floor in Home Assistant and the order is never a guess. The order matters for more than the picture: a signal that goes through a floor loses strength, and PadSpan counts the floors in between from it.

How far apart the floors are is PadSpan's, not Home Assistant's: Setup's floor heights step (chapter 9). A sync from Home Assistant never overwrites them.

Outdoors. A floor called Outside, Outdoor, Outdoors, Exterior, Garden or Yard is treated as the outdoor floor: at ground level, and drawn in the 3D view. The flat map doesn't show rooms on it.

An area with no floor is offered on every floor plan, and the Rooms screen says No floor set in Home Assistant.

A house with one floor and no floors in Home Assistant works as it is: everything is on one floor.

The Rooms screen

Rooms, in the menu, lists every room with what PadSpan keeps for it. The names and floors are read only here; Rename in Home Assistant and Add a room in Home Assistant open Home Assistant's own pages. Each room's row has its name and floor, its colour (Room colour, on every map), and:

FieldWhat it is
OutlineDrawn or Not drawn. Draw outline or Edit outline opens it on its plan in Mapping.
Ceiling height (m)1.5 to 30 m, or blank for the floor's height. Save height.
Split level (m up or down)For a room a few steps up or down: up to 3 m up, 1.5 m down, or blank for level with its floor.
Kind of spaceFor a room on a house floor: indoors, or a covered or open deck or patio. Work it out guesses it from the house.
SurfaceFor an outdoor room: what the 3D view draws on its floor.
ReceiversThe receivers in it. See receivers opens the Receivers screen.
In it nowWho is in it now, or Nobody.
LightsThe lights in it, as the Atlas counts them.

Colour, ceiling height and split level save at once. An outline drawn on a plan goes onto the map of the house only when you choose Put it on the map (chapter 9), never by itself.

Renaming and removing

PadSpan knows a room by its name.

  • A room that isn't an area any more, such as one you renamed or removed in Home Assistant, shows on the Rooms screen as PadSpan only, not a Home Assistant area, with its outline kept. After a rename, draw the outline again for the new name and remove the old one in Mapping → Edit.
  • A receiver moved to another room: change its device's area in Home Assistant, or in PadSpan with Set room on the Overview's Who's where now card.
  • An area you don't want can be removed in Home Assistant, or with Delete in PadSpan's Manage screen, which asks twice. That deletes it from Home Assistant itself, not just PadSpan: every device and entity in it is left with no area.

What PadSpan reads and never changes

PadSpan reads Home Assistant's areas, floors and device registry. It changes them only when you press a button for it, one at a time: Set room or Assign for a receiver, a light's room, Add to Home Assistant for a new area, Delete for an area on Manage. The full list is in the README's What PadSpan never touches.
